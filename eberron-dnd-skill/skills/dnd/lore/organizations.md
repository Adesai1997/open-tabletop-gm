# Organizations, Factions & Power Groups

## Heirs of Dhakaan (Source: Heirs-of-Dhakaan.pdf; Strike-Force_-Dhakaan.pdf)
Remnants of the **Empire of Dhakaan**, the goblinoid civilization that dominated Khorvaire for
millennia until the **Daelkyr** invasion from Xoriat broke it (the **Gatekeeper** druids bound the
Daelkyr in Khyber). As the empire collapsed, great generals took their clans into hiding; they have
re-emerged with the rise of **Darguun** and could unseat **Lhesh Haruuc** — or threaten the Five
Nations — if they united. They don't: no clan agrees who is the true heir of Dhakaan.

**Unusual racial cooperation** (unlike Ghaal'dar/Marguul goblinoids where the strong rule the weak):
- **Hobgoblins** rule through discipline; typical soldier is a **2nd-level fighter** specialized as
  infantry/archer/cavalry. Female hobgoblins are the **duur'kala** ("dirge singers") — rare bard
  spellcasters who inspire troops and heal; spiritual leaders. Alchemy/healing/diplomacy are female
  arts.
- **Bugbears** are raised as heroes/shock troops; nearly all are **barbarians** (Rage = cultivated
  battle-fury, not savagery).
- **Goblins** are the largest class (peasants, tradesmen, and increasingly wizards/artificers among
  the **Kech Volaar**). Two all-goblin clans: **taarka'khesh** ("silent wolves," scout rogues/rangers)
  and **shaarat'khesh** ("silent blades," monk/rogue spies & assassins — see religions.md).

**Clans named:** **Kech Volaar** (the more reasonable "word-bearers," led by **Tuura Dhakaan**),
**Kech Sharaat** (aggressive; swordsmen following a samurai-like tradition), plus hero **Ruus
Dhakaan**. Relics sought include the **Crown of Lhesh Dhakaan** and the **Breastplate of Khamvuul
Norek**. **Tactics** (Strike-Force_-Dhakaan.pdf): precise, adaptive small-unit combat; snipers use
Plunging Shot; bugbears rage in first, hobgoblin troopers trip/harangue with Combat Expertise/Improved
Trip; the dirge singer casts *hold person*/*confusion* and inspires. **Weaknesses:** poor Will saves
and **no divine magic** (agnostic culture) — a *fireball* or an undead foe hurts them.

## Children of Khyber & House Tarkanan (Source: The-Children-of-Khyber.pdf)
Bearers of **aberrant dragonmarks**, feared since the **War of the Mark** (when aberrant lords wielded
madness, flame, and plague and were put down by the pure houses). In the last few years aberrant marks
have grown in size/power for the first time in 1,000+ years — the "children of Khyber." Most are
loners; many are cynical/ruthless (truly noble souls cannot become children of Khyber). See
prestige-classes.md for the PrC. **House Tarkanan** is a criminal guild (Sharn-based) organized as a
mocking mirror of a dragonmarked house, recruiting and training aberrant heirs and preparing for a
"second War of the Mark." Matriarch **Thora Tavin** (LE human rogue 6/assassin 6/child of Khyber 2)
can sense aberrant marks. Enforcer/assassin **Rotting Bal** (human monk 6/child of Khyber 4, CR 10) is
her devoted killer, trained by a dolgaunt in the Shadow Marches (see bestiary.md).

## Dreaming Dark / Blades of the Quori (Source: Blades-of-the-Quori.pdf; Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf)
The **Inspired** (human vessels ridden by **quori** spirits from **Dal Quor**) wage a 1,000+-year war
to exterminate the **kalashtar**; their covert arm is the **Dreaming Dark**. In Khorvaire they rely on
stealth, intrigue, and possessed agents ("anyone could be an agent of the Dreaming Dark"). Quori
warriors fight with **mind blades** and psionics rather than steel; martial traditions (kalashtar and
Inspired alike): **jilashtora** (soulknife+monk "flowing blades"), **ojilashta** (soulknife+psychic
warrior "bladeshapers"), **tashalantora** (monk+psychic warrior, = Fist of Zuoken). The kalashtar
martial art is **sheshan talarash dasyannah** ("the path of shadows"). The Dreaming Dark may secretly
train Khorvairian psions as future quori host-bodies. See psionics.md and races-cultures.md.

## Daughters of Sora Kell (Source: Droaam-Hordes-Press-Graywall-Defenders.pdf)
Three hags who seized western Breland after the 987 withdrawal and rule the monster nation of
**Droaam**; they direct hordes of gnolls, ogres, minotaurs, trolls, and medusas against the Graywall
passes.

## Eldeen Wolves (Source: Eldeen-Wolves-Seize-Expedition.pdf; Relief-Convoy-Attacked-in-Aundair.pdf)
Guerrilla insurgents in western Aundair / eastern Eldeen Reaches led by the mysterious **Rhunadask**,
demanding Aundair quit the Wynarn River Valley; they kidnap, sabotage, and murder (e.g. seizing a
University of Wynarn expedition in 998). Distinct from the Ashbound druid **Amleerin the Fox**, though
both operate along the Wynarn.

## The Aurum (Source: Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf)
A secretive society of wealthy, ambitious "concordians," strongest among the Mror; honorable dwarves
say "true Mror are iron and gold, but those who serve the Aurum are gold alone." Pursues secret,
deadly vendettas; some say it is effectively a clan of its own. Conspires (with the Chamber and King
Kaius) in high-level plots (Source: Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

## The Chamber (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf)
An ancient, powerful faction of dragons manipulating the draconic Prophecy; excellent
counterintelligence (Phiarlan cannot spy on it). Conspires alongside the Aurum and King Kaius.

## Lords of Dust (Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1/-Part-2.pdf; Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf)
Ancient rakshasa/fiend cabal serving the bound overlords; whisper the "false flame" to corrupt Silver
Flame priests; a possible hidden power behind Riedra. Beyond Phiarlan's reach.

## Lord of Blades (Source: Airship-Action.pdf; Guile-the-Warforged-Ninja.pdf)
Messianic **warforged** leader in the Mournland who preaches warforged supremacy and the elimination
of the "flesh-and-blood" races; commands warforged archers/wizards; has stolen airships (Riedran
crysteel, adamantine cargoes) and may have recruited the assassin **Guile**. Scales in power with the
party, like the Dhakaani heroes (Source: Heirs-of-Dhakaan.pdf analogy).

## House of Harpies (Source: 06-House-Of-Harpies.pdf)
A thieves'-guild lair concealed in an old evergreen in the forest outside a town — a self-contained
locale/encounter site (the guild predates on the town while druids/rangers look elsewhere). Usable as
a rogues' hideout or harpy-infested dungeon. Retrieve at runtime for the map/stat details.

## Order of the Emerald Claw (Emerald Claw)
Militant Blood-of-Vol/Karrnath-linked order; Phiarlan has spies within it; appears as airship
kidnappers in hooks (Sources: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf; Airship-Action.pdf).

## Order of the Chalice (Source: D_D-Complete-Warrior.pdf)
Demon-hunting knightly order (mostly paladins) devoted to eradicating demons on the Material Plane and
in the Abyss; strategy is to remove the demon princes so the Abyss collapses into infighting. A
*Complete Warrior* web-enhancement organization (see prestige-classes.md / rules-supplements.md).

## Boromar Clan & other criminal groups
Halfling-led crime syndicate of Sharn; a primary source of thieves/assassins alongside House Tarkanan
(Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf). Salvage gangs like **Ikar the Black**'s operate around New
Cyre/the Mournland (Source: Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).
