# Dragonmarked Houses

The thirteen dragonmarked houses are Khorvaire's mercantile-magical dynasties. The corpus gives
**deep** detail on House Phiarlan (a 3-part series) and scattered facts on the others; the full
roster below is completed from house references named across the corpus. Where the corpus does not
supply a fact, retrieve the *Dragonmarked* sourcebook at runtime.

## House Phiarlan — Mark of Shadow (elves) — espionage & the arts
**The most detailed house in the library.** Origins trace to Xen'drik, where elf "spirit keepers"
(*phiarlans*) served as liaisons/morale-keepers/spies among elven sects during the Elven Uprising
against the giants; they joined the exodus to Aerenal (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf). Under
the Undying Court they became true spies; the **Mark of Shadow** first appeared on the **Elorrenthi**
line and spread to the **Shol** and **Tialaen** lines. After the extermination of the line of Vol
(and fear that shadow-marked elves were next), most fled Aerenal to Khorvaire, forming **House
Phiarlan** (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf).

- **Symbol:** a five-headed hydra (one head per artistic demesne); secondary symbol the silver
  "mask of shadows." Overall a **neutral** organization seeing itself as an agent of balance
  (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Two.pdf).
- **Guild:** Artisans and Entertainers Guild. Certification requires ≥8 ranks in a Perform art.
- **The Five Demesnes** (artistic disciplines + host cities) (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Two.pdf):
  - **Memory** — written word, propaganda; **Fairhaven, Aundair**.
  - **Motion** — dance, acrobatics, escorts, burglary-adjacent arts; **Korth, Karrnath**.
  - **Music** — song/instruments; **Flamekeep, Thrane**.
  - **Shape** — physical craft (costumes, props, sculpture, finest woodwork outside Aerenal);
    originally **Metrol, Cyre**, lost in the Mourning, re-established in **Wroat, Breland**.
  - **Shadow** — deception, illusion, stealth, oratory; **Sharn, Breland** (oldest surviving
    demesne; home of the house matriarch), overseen by **Baron Elvinor Elorrenthi d'Phiarlan**.
- **Carnival of Shadows:** a traveling circus (three companies) reaching even Lhazaar and Talenta;
  Serpentine agents often ride with it.
- **The Serpentine Table** — the secret **sixth demesne**, the espionage/assassination arm; its
  citadel's location is a DM secret. Agent ranks & monthly retainers (Source:
  House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf):
  - **Shadow** (≥7 ranks in 3 Serpentine skills) — 10 gp/mo.
  - **Wraith** (≥9 ranks in 4 skills) — 50 gp/mo.
  - **Spectre** (≥12 ranks in 4 skills) — 200 gp/mo.
  - **Ghost** (≥15 ranks in 4 skills) — 1,000 gp/mo; only a handful exist.
  - **Lords of the Serpentine Table** — top circle; centuries of service required; only elves ever
    admitted. Serpentine skills: Bluff, Disguise, Gather Information, Hide, Knowledge (any), Listen,
    Move Silently, Open Lock, Search, Spot.
- **Service pricing:** see world-primer.md (secrets 10–100 gp; scrying base 280 gp; surveillance
  ~2,000 gp/day). The house prefers powerful clients and never kills/kidnaps a client in good
  standing; it would rather abduct than kill (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf).
- **Rival intelligence networks:** the **Trust of Zilargo** rivals Phiarlan; Phiarlan has spies
  inside the Aurum, Order of the Emerald Claw, and the Twelve, but not inside the Lords of Dust,
  Dreaming Dark, or the Chamber (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf).
- **Tyasha d'Phiarlan** is cited as an example house savant (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Two.pdf).

## House Thuranni — Mark of Shadow (elves)
Split from Phiarlan in the "Thuranni feud"; also elves of shadow and espionage. Some independents
broke away during that feud (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf; House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf).

## House Lyrandar — Mark of Storm (half-elves) — sea & sky
Operates the **airship** fleet and sea vessels; heirs feel "a deeper bond with wind and water"
(Source: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf). Standard airships ~300 ft; expanding towers to
Stormreach, Adderport, Keth, Sigilstar, Starilaskur under scion **Yarrick d'Lyrandar**; ships named
*River of Stars*, *Virtue's Price*; spokesman **Korvik d'Lyrandar**; based at **Stormhome** (Sources:
House-Lyrandar-Mulls-New-Airship-Routes.pdf; Lyrandar-Investigates-Mysterious-Lights.pdf). Buys hulls
from Zilargo yards like the **Power of Purity** (founder **Harkra Loivaerl Lonadar**) (Source:
Banditry-Delays-Airships-Maiden-Voyage.pdf). Ran a joint relief convoy with the Diresharks fleet at
the Cathanikau eruption (Source: Lhazaar-Volcano-Erupts.pdf).

## House Tharashk — Mark of Finding (humans/half-orcs, Shadow Marches)
The **Finder's Guild**: inquisitives (private investigators) and dragonshard prospectors. Announced
dragonshard ventures in the Lhazaar Principalities from its Shadow Marches compound at **Zarash'ak**;
triumvir **Khundar'aashta**; the **Aashta** family leads. For minor spy work, Phiarlan tells clients
to "hire a Tharashk inquisitive" (Sources: Lhazaar-Investment-Pacts-Announced.pdf;
House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf). Uses apostrophe-heavy Marches naming (Source: Reptilian... none — from
Tharashk articles).

## House Cannith — Mark of Making (humans) — artifice
The great manufacturing house; its **foundries created the warforged**. Guile the warforged was
"stolen from the Cannith foundry at creation" (Source: Guile-the-Warforged-Ninja.pdf). A Cannith heir,
**Jaxon d'Cannith**, appears as a foil to aberrant-marked characters ("Your sigil creates… mine holds
the power to destroy") (Source: The-Children-of-Khyber.pdf). Cannith heir **Ilya d'Cannith** was a
Dragonslayer-sniper victim (Source: Dragonslayer-Sniper-Claims-Sixth-Victim.pdf).

## House Kundarak — Mark of Warding (dwarves) — banking & security
Runs the banking guild and warded vaults; House **Sivis** gnomes "uncovered the full potential of the
Mark of Warding and helped Clan Kundarak find its place among the dragonmark houses," and Sivis
notaries/stonesenders staff the Kundarak banking guild (Source: Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf).
Kundarak has never sat on the Mror council of clans, being the house set to "watch" the exiled
dwarves (Source: Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf). A Kundarak noble was a Dragonslayer victim,
and Kundarak lending practices drew the sniper's ire (Source: Dragonslayer-Sniper-Claims-Sixth-Victim.pdf).

## House Deneith — Mark of Sentinel (humans) — mercenaries & protection
Provides the **Blademark** (mercenaries) and the **Sentinel Marshals** (bounty-hunting lawmen with
cross-border authority). Sentinel Marshals hunt the Eldeen Wolves' captives and were expected to
catch the Dragonslayer sniper (Sources: Eldeen-Wolves-Seize-Expedition.pdf;
Dragonslayer-Sniper-Claims-Sixth-Victim.pdf). The **Karrnathi line of House Deneith** has close ties
to the **Order of the Broken Blade**, with swordbrothers among the Blademark and Sentinel Marshals
(Source: Monastic-Orders.pdf).

## House Sivis — Mark of Scribing (gnomes, Zilargo)
Communication (message stones/speaking stones), notaries, translation. Strong ties to Kundarak (see
above). A Sivis heir, **Torlak d'Sivis**, was a Dragonslayer victim (Source:
Dragonslayer-Sniper-Claims-Sixth-Victim.pdf). Sivis gnomes helped shape Mror mercantile culture
(Source: Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf).

## House Medani — Mark of Detection (half-elves) — the Warning Guard
Produced the **dragonshard foci that could detect lycanthropes**, which helped end the Purge (Source:
Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf).

## House Jorasco — Mark of Healing (halflings)
Healing services guild. Heir **Chenni d'Jorasco** was a Dragonslayer victim in Sharn (Source:
Dragonslayer-Sniper-Claims-Sixth-Victim.pdf).

## House Ghallanda — Mark of Hospitality (halflings)
Inns, food, shelter (Hostelers Guild). Heir **Lasca d'Ghallanda** was a Dragonslayer victim (Source:
Dragonslayer-Sniper-Claims-Sixth-Victim.pdf).

## House Orien — Mark of Passage (humans) — overland transport
Runs the **lightning rail** and courier/teleport services; the House Orien trade road links Fairhaven
and Ghalt through Lathleer in Aundair (Sources: Mysterious-Disappearances.pdf; Faith-and-Deed.pdf).

## House Vadalis — Mark of Handling (humans) — magebred beasts
"Members of House Vadalis sometimes feel the call of the wild" (druids); the house is associated with
magebred animals (druids/rangers cannot take a magebred creature as an animal companion) (Source:
Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).

## Related bodies
- **The Twelve** — the arcane consortium of the houses; Phiarlan keeps spies within it (Source:
  House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf).
- **Aberrant marks / House Tarkanan** — the criminal "shadow" of a dragonmarked house; see
  organizations.md and prestige-classes.md (Child of Khyber).
- The **War of the Mark** was the ancient conflict in which aberrant-marked lords were defeated by
  the pure houses (Source: The-Children-of-Khyber.pdf).

### Dragonmarks & druids (mechanics)
A druid keeps a visible dragonmark while in wild shape (as a pattern on fur/hide) and can use its
powers, but the mark is **not heritable** through an assumed animal form; spotting a mark on an
animal is a Spot check (base DC 15, modified by creature and mark size; +8 in elemental form). A
dragonmarked druid can hide the mark with the *thousand faces* ability (Source:
Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).
