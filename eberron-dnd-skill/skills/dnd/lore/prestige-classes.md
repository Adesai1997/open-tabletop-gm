## Child of Khyber (Source: The-Children-of-Khyber.pdf)
A 5-level PrC for characters bearing **aberrant dragonmarks**. Draws on *Dragonmarked* (Lesser/Greater
Aberrant Dragonmark feats). Thematically: the power chooses the vessel; one day the aberrant mark
grows and the character *becomes* a child of Khyber (not chosen/trained).

- **Hit Die:** d8. **Alignment:** any nongood.
- **Requirements:** any two skills at 7 ranks; feat **Aberrant Dragonmark**.
- **Class skills (4 + Int/level):** Bluff, Climb, Concentration, Disable Device, Disguise, Forgery,
  Gather Information, Hide, Intimidate, Knowledge (local), Jump, Listen, Move Silently, Open Lock,
  Search, Sense Motive, Sleight of Hand, Spot, Survival, Tumble.
- **Progression (BAB / Fort / Ref / Will / Special):**
  - 1: +0 / +2 / +2 / +2 — Aberrant affinity; Lesser Aberrant Dragonmark (bonus feat)
  - 2: +1 / +3 / +3 / +3 — Additional action points (+2/level max); improved aberrant dragonmark
  - 3: +2 / +3 / +3 / +3 — Improved lesser aberrant dragonmark
  - 4: +3 / +4 / +4 / +4 — Greater Aberrant Dragonmark (bonus feat)
  - 5: +3 / +4 / +4 / +4 — Aberrant burst
- **Features:** **Aberrant affinity** (+class level to Cha checks vs aberrations / aberrant-marked
  people); **Lesser/Greater Aberrant Dragonmark** as bonus feats (skip skill prereqs); **improved
  (lesser) aberrant dragonmark** (+1 use/day of the mark's spell-like ability); **aberrant burst**
  (1/day boost a mark power's CL and save DC by +3, but be sickened 1d4 rounds).
- **Recruiter:** House Tarkanan (see organizations.md). Sample NPC: **Rotting Bal** (see bestiary.md).

## The Body Leech (Source: The-Body-Leech-Prestige-Class.pdf) — revised 9/27/2004
A 5-level **evil psionic** PrC that traps victims in cocoons and drains their ability scores for power
points ("every person is a cognizance crystal waiting to be captured").

- **Hit Die:** d4. **Alignment:** any evil.
- **Requirements:** Bluff 8 ranks, Diplomacy 8 ranks; feats **Body Fuel, Inquisitor**; must manifest
  **ectoplasmic cocoon** and **mindswitch**.
- **Class skills (4 + Int/level):** Bluff, Concentration, Craft, Diplomacy, Disguise, Forgery, Gather
  Information, Handle Animal, Intimidate, Knowledge (psionics), Profession, Psicraft, Sense Motive.
- **Progression (BAB / Fort / Ref / Will / Special / Powers):**
  - 1: +0/+0/+0/+2 — Drain cocoon, stasis cocoon — (no manifesting advance)
  - 2: +1/+0/+0/+3 — Create tether hound — +1 manifester level
  - 3: +1/+1/+1/+3 — Stay conscious (Diehard) — +1 manifester level
  - 4: +2/+1/+1/+4 — Greater stasis cocoon — (no advance)
  - 5: +3/+1/+1/+4 — Cheat death — +1 manifester level
- **Drain Cocoon (Su):** free action; deal chosen Str/Dex/Con damage to a cocooned victim (Will save
  DC 10 + ½ char level + Wis mod); gain **2 pp per 3 points** of ability damage; points last 1 + Wis
  mod rounds; one victim/round; a stat at 0 stops draining.
- **Cocoon Drains table (level → max host size / # cocoons / max link distance):** 1 → Medium / 2 /
  1,000 ft; 2 → Medium / 4 / 1 mile; 3 → Large* / 6 / 5 miles; 4 → Large* / 10 / 25 miles; 5 → Huge* /
  16** / 125 miles (*if augmented; **one may be a cheat-death host).
- **Cheat Death (Su):** 1/day, when reduced to ≤0 hp, DC 25 Will save to swap souls into a designated
  cocooned host (works like *true mind switch*, free, if the host's HD are low enough).
- **New powers included:** *Stasis Cocoon* (Leech 3; encases an unconscious creature 1 day/level;
  strands hardness 17, 30 hp; victim dies if not freed before it expires), *Create Tether Hound*
  (Leech 4; builds a guardian construct on a cocoon — see bestiary.md), *Greater Stasis Cocoon*
  (Leech 5; as stasis cocoon but target need not be unconscious, Will negates).

## Psychic Theurge (Source: Psychic-Theurge-Prestige-Class.pdf)
A 10-level PrC combining **divine spellcasting** with **psionics** (analogous to the mystic theurge /
cerebremancer). "Feels right at home in Eberron"; the **church of Sardior** hosts many.

- **Hit Die:** d4. **Skill points:** 2 + Int/level.
- **Requirements:** Knowledge (psionics) 6 ranks, Knowledge (religion) 6 ranks; able to cast **2nd-
  level divine spells**; able to manifest **2nd-level powers**.
- **Class skills:** Concentration, Craft, Decipher Script, Knowledge (psionics/religion/the planes),
  Profession, Psicraft, Spellcraft.
- **No** weapon/armor proficiencies.
- **Progression:** every one of the 10 levels grants **+1 level of an existing divine spellcasting
  class** *and* **+1 level of an existing manifesting class** (spells/day, caster level, power points,
  powers known, manifester level) — but no other class benefits (bonus feats, psicrystal abilities,
  etc.). BAB +0→+5; saves Fort/Ref +0→+3, Will +2→+7 over 10 levels.

## Divine prestige classes — *More Divinity* (Source: More-Divinity.pdf)
A *Complete Divine* web enhancement presenting **four new divine PrCs** that didn't fit the book. The
first is the **Dweomerkeeper** — "shepherds of arcana," priests who blend divine faith with arcane
magic. (Retrieve More-Divinity.pdf at runtime for the full four write-ups and tables.)

## Order of the Chalice knights — *Complete Warrior* (Source: D_D-Complete-Warrior.pdf)
A web enhancement expanding the **Knight of the Chalice** organization (from *Complete Warrior*): the
demon-hunting **Order of the Chalice**, its chapter house, and three fully-statted NPC members (mostly
paladins with Knight of the Chalice levels). See organizations.md.
