# Races & Cultures

## Warforged
Living constructs built by **House Cannith** foundries as Last War soldiers; bodies include "fibrous
strands not unlike the roots of a tree," making them **living constructs** (Source:
Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).
- **Warforged druids:** a rare path. Adamantine Body and Mithril Body feats block druidic abilities
  (like metal armor); only **Ironwood Body** (Races of Eberron) allows improved armor + full druid
  power. Goodberry and wild shape are *not* healing-subschool, so they benefit a warforged fully. In
  wild shape a warforged is no longer a construct (loses immunities/armor bonus, gains healing-spell
  benefit, can't be repaired). They gravitate to the **Wardens of the Wood**; elder **Gatekeepers**
  are interested in these "possibly immortal guardians." The Mournland **Followers of the Broken Path**
  are a warforged druid sect healing the dead-gray land (Source: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).
- **Warforged and the Lord of Blades:** the messianic warforged of the Mournland (see organizations.md).
- **Guile** (Source: Guile-the-Warforged-Ninja.pdf): a warforged **ninja**, stolen from a Cannith
  foundry at creation by agents of the **church of Shadow** (House Phiarlan/Thuranni shadow tradition),
  awakened and trained in the Byeshk Mountains as an assassin-messenger. During the war he slipped
  behind enemy lines to kill leaders and sow chaos; failed a mission to kill the rulers of Breland and
  Cyre, then vanished into the newly formed Mournland. Theorized to now serve the Lord of Blades while
  keeping faith in the Shadow. Cold, methodical; avoids mass combat; exploits the fact that he needs
  no rest/food to strike resting or exhausted targets. (Full scaling stat blocks — see bestiary.md.)
- **Warforged cannot be reincarnated** (a warforged can be reincarnated *as* another race) (Source:
  Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).

## Kalashtar
Human-quori symbiotes: humans permanently bonded to renegade **quori** dream-spirits that fled the
Dreaming Dark. Their union grants psionic strength ("My union gives me strength"); they follow the
**Path of Light** and practice the martial art **sheshan talarash dasyannah** ("path of shadows")
(Source: Blades-of-the-Quori.pdf). Homeland is **Adar**, Sarlona; refugee enclaves exist as far as
Cathanikau Island in Lhazaar (Sources: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf; Lhazaar-Volcano-Erupts.pdf). The
Inspired hunt them relentlessly. **Optional non-psionic rule:** kalashtar (and Inspired empty vessels)
can be natural sorcerers instead of psions — favored class sorcerer, +2 effective Cha for bonus arcane
spells, and a *mindlink* 1/day spell-like ability (Source: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf). A reincarnated
kalashtar who returns as any non-kalashtar race **loses her quori bond** (traumatic); a "human" result
instead returns her as kalashtar (Source: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).

## Inspired
Human "empty vessels" of Sarlona bred to host quori spirits; rulers of **Riedra** and agents of the
**Dreaming Dark**. Gifted psions; follow quori martial traditions matching their spirit type
(du'ulora = raw power; tsucora = cunning) (Sources: Blades-of-the-Quori.pdf; Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf).

## Changelings
Shapeshifters; can be reincarnation results; feature in noir intrigue (impersonation, the Cabinet of
Faces). A changeling can be adopted into the goblin **shaarat'khesh** (Source: Monastic-Orders.pdf).
Hooks show changeling identity/expression subtleties and prejudice (e.g. a changeling magewright Arz
framed for airship theft; a changeling unjustly burned in Thrane sparking a revolt) (Sources:
Airship-Action.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf; Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).

## Shifters
Descended (per most legends) from lycanthropes — but shifter moonspeakers reverse it: the moon
**Olarune** made the first lycanthropes *from* shifters, and a hidden thirteenth "dark moon"
corrupted them. Shifters do **not** carry lycanthropy, yet hundreds were massacred during the Purge;
many have never forgiven the Silver Flame or humanity. Some shifter tribes hunt evil lycanthropes
(Source: Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf). Common among the **Shadows of the Forest** druids (Source:
Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf).

## Mror dwarves (Ironroot Mountains)
Passionate, proud, honor-bound; "harder than stone and sharper than steel." Fierce negotiators who
favor **intimidation over diplomacy**; a Mror lord's oath is "as unbreakable as adamantine" (a known
oathbreaker is scorned). They **fall in love with fine objects** and spend more on clothing, armor,
jewelry, and weapons than on their homes; armor is enameled/engraved, weapons personalized, jewelry
signals rank (recognize significance: Int check DC 5, or Knowledge (nobility) DC 20). Architecture is
stark/functional (darkvision, few windows). In battle they favor heavy armor, Power Attack, named
weapons/shields, and battlecries; elite dwarves take fighter/**kensai**/knight-protector paths; **Clan
Droranath** fields true barbarians and frenzied berserkers; **dwarven defenders** guard Kundarak
vaults. Arcane magic is a recent import via **Zilargo gnome immigrants** (translators, mediators,
tailors, bankers' clerks) — the ancient runesmith/artificer secrets were lost in exile but are being
reclaimed. Twelve clans (see nations.md). Patron gods: **Kol Korran** and **Onatar** (Source:
Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf).

## Elves — Aereni, Tairnadal (Valenar), and the houses of shadow
Elves fled fallen **Xen'drik** to **Aerenal**. Three paths toward conquering death diverged: the
**Priests of Transition** (→ the deathless **Undying Court**), the blood-magic **line of Vol**
(exterminated), and the martial **Tairnadal** (who become vessels for ancestor-heroes → the **Valenar**
elves). Phiarlan/Thuranni descend from the shadow lines **Tialaen, Shol, Elorrenthi** (Sources:
House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf; nations.md). Valenar blood guardians (druids) and the secular **Jaeldira**
martial monks reflect the warrior culture (Sources: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One/Two.pdf;
Monastic-Orders.pdf).

## Drow (Xen'drik)
Native to Xen'drik; regard Khorvairian explorers as **poachers** (e.g. of dream serpents). Underdark
drow houses enslave others — e.g. the maur giant **Drashan Daverund** is a drow house's slave-warrior
(Sources: Dream-Serpent-Hides-Become-Fashion.pdf; Drashan-Daverund.pdf).

## Goblinoids (Dhakaani vs. modern)
See organizations.md (Heirs of Dhakaan) for the disciplined Dhakaani hobgoblin/bugbear/goblin culture
and religions.md (Shaarat'khesh) for their assassins. Modern degraded goblinoids include the
**Ghaal'dar** and **Marguul** (bugbear tribes), where the strong simply tyrannize the weak (Sources:
Heirs-of-Dhakaan.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

## Other peoples named in the corpus
- **Talenta halflings** — dinosaur-riding nomads (nations.md).
- **Sarlonan psionic races** — duergar, dromites, elans, maenads, half-giants (Source:
  Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf).
- **Orcs** — strong druidic ties (Gatekeeper roots in the Shadow Marches); Jhorash'tar orc raiders
  harry the Mror Holds (Sources: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf; Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf).
- **Reincarnation weightings** (Eberron druid table) run from bugbear/gnoll/orc (Str-favoring) to
  kobold/halfling/elf (Dex-favoring), including changeling and shifter (Source:
  Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).
