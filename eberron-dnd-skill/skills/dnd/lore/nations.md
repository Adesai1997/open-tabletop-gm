# Nations & Regions of Khorvaire (and beyond)

Facts distilled from the corpus. Rulers, borders, and character below are attributed; retrieve the
*Eberron Campaign Setting* / *Five Nations* books at runtime for full canon.

## The Five Nations (successor states of Galifar)

### Breland
The largest, most populous of the Five Nations; capital **Wroat**, greatest city **Sharn** (see
world-primer.md). Ruled by **King Boranel** (King Boranel ir'Wynarn); his fourth son is **Prince
Aejar "the scarred"** (lost an arm and bears claw scars, still adventurous), lost climbing Mount
Herrian in the Blackcaps (Source: Prince-Aejars-Climbing-Expedition-to-Mount-Herrian-Overdue.pdf).
Breland **withdrew from western Breland in 987**, ceding ground to what became Droaam, and built 13
fortifications after the 990 **Graywall Levy** (Source: Droaam-Hordes-Press-Graywall-Defenders.pdf).
Military assets: the **7th Army**, the floating fortress **Argonth**, Borumel's War Council, the
**Knight Rangers** of the King's Forest, and intelligence agency the **King's Dark Lanterns** (who
may be training psionic *lurks*) (Sources: Droaam-Hordes-Press-Graywall-Defenders.pdf;
Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf; Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf). The **King's Forest** rainforest in
southeastern Breland is patrolled by druid-rangers, the **Shadows of the Forest** (five bands of
~25, tied to displacer beasts) (Source: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf).

### Aundair
The nation of arcane magic ("Aundair places its trust in arcane magic") (Source:
Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf). Capital **Fairhaven**; ruled by **Queen Aurala** (with the **Royal Eyes of
Aundair** intelligence service). Stronghold of the **puritan** faction of the Silver Flame (Source:
Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf). Fights guerrilla insurgents along the Wynarn River: the **Ashbound**
druid arsonist **Amleerin the Fox** (captured, tried at Fairhaven) and the **Eldeen Wolves** under
**Rhunadask**, operating from the Eldeen Reaches (Sources: Aundair-Army-Captures-Rebel-Leader.pdf;
Eldeen-Wolves-Seize-Expedition.pdf; Relief-Convoy-Attacked-in-Aundair.pdf). Places: **Wyr** (army HQ),
**Otharaunt Fortress**, **Salanoux**, **Windshire**, **Lathleer** (on the Orien road, temple of
Boldrei), **Lake Galifar** (Sources: as above; Faith-and-Deed.pdf).

### Thrane
Theocracy of the **Church of the Silver Flame**; capital/seat **Flamekeep**, led by the **Keeper of
the Flame** (currently **Jaela Daran**; a past Keeper **Jolan Sol** proclaimed lycanthropy corrupted
the soul in 832 YK) (Source: Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf; Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-2.pdf).
Fortress-monasteries: **Kloijnir** (watches Flamekeep), **Tira's Watch** (Thrane/Breland border),
**Morningcrest**. Secular knightly bodies include the **Knights of Thrane** (Source:
Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-2.pdf). "Thrane relies on the divine might of the Silver Flame"
(Source: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf).

### Karrnath
Martial nation; ruled by **King Kaius** (Kaius III) (Source: Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf). Home of the
**Blood of Vol** temples and (historically) undead legions. Cities: **Korth** (Phiarlan Demesne of
Motion), **Karrlakton** and **Rekkenmark** (Order of the Broken Blade monasteries) (Sources:
House-Phiarlan-Part-Two.pdf; Monastic-Orders.pdf). May be exploring psionic **kineticists** as war
assets; the **Red Gauntlet Regiment** is a Karrnathi unit (Sources: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf;
Red-Gauntlet-Regiment-Reunites-in-Sharn.pdf — retrieve at runtime).

### Cyre → the Mournland
Destroyed on the Day of Mourning (see world-primer.md). Old capital **Metrol**. Survivors are Cyran
refugees; **New Cyre** is a resettlement (Sources: House-Phiarlan-Part-Two.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

## Other Khorvairian powers

### Droaam
A monster nation in the west, seized after Breland's 987 withdrawal and ruled by the hags **Daughters
of Sora Kell**. Hordes of minotaurs, ogres, gnolls, trolls, and medusas press the Breland army at the
**Graywall** passes (a 2,000+-warrior assault in 998 captured Mt. Anglio, Virtue Soars, and Blackwood
Cleft). Capital **Great Crag** (a Flayed Hand monastery). Border town **Ardev** (Brelish side)
(Sources: Droaam-Hordes-Press-Graywall-Defenders.pdf; Monastic-Orders.pdf;
Report-Critical-Gaps-Exist-in-Graywall-Defenses.pdf — retrieve at runtime).

### Mror Holds
Dwarven confederacy in the **Ironroot Mountains**, unified relatively recently. The **twelve great
clans** (descended from twelve exiled heroes): **Mroranon, Doldarun, Droranath, Kolkarun, Kundarak,
Laranak, Londurak, Narathun, Noldrun, Soldorak, Soranath, Toldorath, Tordannon** — Kundarak sits
apart (watcher), and **Clan Noldrun was destroyed**. Passionate, honor-bound, fierce negotiators who
prize fine craftsmanship and personal appearance; **Clan Droranath** has true barbarians/frenzied
berserkers. Gnome immigrants from Zilargo handle bookkeeping/tailoring; the **Aurum** (see
organizations.md) is influential and distrusted. The volcanic **Fist of Onatar** in the southern
Ironroots is a sacred site — the corpus notes a **conflict**: the *Eberron Campaign Setting* says
**Clan Mroranon** controls it, while the *Eberron Player's Guide* places it in **Clan Soldorak**'s
hold (Sources: Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf; A-Place-to-Call-Home.pdf). The 881 YK eruption
of **Jaurakhti Mountain** occurred here (Source: Lhazaar-Volcano-Erupts.pdf). Orc raiders of the
**Jhorash'tar** are a constant threat (Source: Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf). Places:
**Krona Peak**, **Korunda Gate**, **Vonmalk** (Sources: Monastic-Orders.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf;
A-Place-to-Call-Home.pdf).

### Lhazaar Principalities
Northeastern confederacy of sea barons/merchant princes; port cities **Regalport** and **Port Verge**.
Princes named: **Ryger ir'Wynarn** (Seadragons fleet), **Kaelisk** (Coldtide Fleet, Stonespur Island).
Fleets: **Seadragons, Diresharks, Coldtide**. Retains old Sarlonan traditions (a possible home for
psionic *Six Hidden Houses*). **Mount Cathanikau** (an island volcano ~500 mi NE of Port Verge,
holding a kalashtar refugee enclave and an Onatar shrine) erupted in 998; local Hierophant Druid
**Tybalt Orenn** sought to calm it. Legendary lost pirate treasure: **Hoard of the Twin Drakes**
(Drake brothers died 945) (Sources: Lhazaar-Investment-Pacts-Announced.pdf; Lhazaar-Volcano-Erupts.pdf;
Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf).

### Zilargo
Gnome nation famed for the **Trust** (secret police/intelligence rivaling Phiarlan) and for airship
engineering (the Power of Purity yard; elemental-binding research). Cities: **Korranberg** (Library of
Korranberg), **Trolanport**, **Zalanberg**. The **Howling Peaks** border it (bandit country)
(Sources: House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf; Banditry-Delays-Airships-Maiden-Voyage.pdf; Dream-Serpent-Hides-Become-Fashion.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

### Eldeen Reaches
Druid-influenced western nation that won independence from Aundair (border traditionally the western
Wynarn). Home of the five druid sects and the great awakened greatpine **Oalian**; the **Towering
Wood** lies within. Insurgent **Eldeen Wolves** under Rhunadask raid across the Wynarn (Sources:
Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf; Eldeen-Wolves-Seize-Expedition.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

### Talenta Plains
Home of the nomadic **halflings** who ride dinosaurs (e.g. clawfoot dinosaurs); reached even by the
Carnival of Shadows (Sources: House-Phiarlan-Part-Two.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

### Darguun
Hobgoblin nation founded by **Lhesh Haruuc**; hides the resurgent **Heirs of Dhakaan** clans. Capital
**Rukhaan Draal** (a Flayed Hand monastery). Contains the **Seven Caves** and Dhakaani ruins
(Sources: Heirs-of-Dhakaan.pdf; Monastic-Orders.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

### Q'barra
Frontier jungle land with ancient ruins raided by Morgrave University expeditions (Source:
Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

### Valenar
Nation of the **Tairnadal** elves — the warrior line that seeks to become vessels for fallen ancestors
through martial perfection; Valenar druids (blood guardians / **Jaeldira** martial monks) and Valenar
riding horses (Sources: House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf; Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf/Two;
Monastic-Orders.pdf).

### Aerenal
Island homeland of the elves and the deathless **Undying Court** (see races-cultures.md & religions.md).
Ancient enemy of the dragons of **Argonnessen**. The line of **Vol** was exterminated here (Source:
House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf). Security forces: **Cairdal Blades**, the **Deathguard** (Source:
House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf).

## Beyond Khorvaire
- **Sarlona** — continent of the mind; seat of psionic power; homeland of **kalashtar** and
  **Inspired**; also duergar, dromites, elans, maenads, half-giants. Split into **Riedra** (Inspired-
  ruled empire) and **Adar** (mountain refuge of the kalashtar/Path of Light). Once closely tied to
  Dal Quor (Source: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf; Blades-of-the-Quori.pdf).
- **Xen'drik** — ancient giant continent across the sea; source of dream serpents and lost magic;
  the drow regard Khorvairian explorers as poachers; Khorvaire's port there is **Stormreach**
  (Sources: Dream-Serpent-Hides-Become-Fashion.pdf; House-Lyrandar-Mulls-New-Airship-Routes.pdf;
  House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf).
- **The Demon Wastes** — savage druids and fiends; **Festering Holt**; a destination for destroying
  cursed objects (Sources: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).
- **The Shadow Marches** — swampy homeland of House Tarkanan-adjacent monasteries and Tharashk's
  orc/half-orc origins; **Rethild (the Great Swamp)** lies nearby (Sources: The-Children-of-Khyber.pdf;
  Reptilian-Tool-of-Tiamat.pdf).
- **Argonnessen** — continent of the dragons; ancient foe of Aerenal (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf).
- **The Frostfell** — polar wastes with buried giant ruins (Source: Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).
