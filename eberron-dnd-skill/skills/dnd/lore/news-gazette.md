# News Gazette — 998 YK (session hooks)

Every dated in-world news story in the library (all by David Noonan, *Sharn Inquisitive* / regional
datelines), presented chronologically by Galifar month with the facts and a ready-to-run hook. Use
these to open a session or seed a subplot. Cite the source file when used.

## Zarantyr 6, 998 — "Lyrandar Investigates Mysterious Lights" (Sharn)
Mysterious translucent glowing lights (spherical/ovoid, sometimes clustering into a bipedal "glowing
woman") swarmed the airship **Virtue's Price** departing **Lyrandar Tower**, the third such sighting
that week; they streaked back into the **Mithral Tower** district. Lyrandar's **Korvik d'Lyrandar**
downplays it; Morgrave professor **Moira Harnatri** says it's too bright/big/fast for a common
illusion and persisted beyond a ground wizard's range. **Hook:** what are the lights — a manifest-zone
phenomenon, a ghost, a Dreaming Dark scout, or arcane pranksters? (Source: Lyrandar-Investigates-Mysterious-Lights.pdf)

## Zarantyr 8, 998 — "Prince Aejar's Climbing Expedition to Mount Herrian Overdue" (Cragwar)
Two dozen climbers — including **Prince Aejar** (Boranel's scarred, one-armed fourth son), diviner
**Alliqua Brashen**, retired Colonel **Warrick Nathuna**, explorer **Laasha Halli**, a squad of King's
Own infantry, and porters — are two days overdue in the **Blackcap Mountains**. Mount Herrian holds
ancient **Dhakaani-era shrines** and stays warm/snowless. **Hook:** join the rescue; discover what in
the Dhakaani ruins waylaid a royal prince. (Source: Prince-Aejars-Climbing-Expedition-to-Mount-Herrian-Overdue.pdf)

## Therendor 1, 998 — "Droaam Hordes Press Graywall Defenders" (Ardev)
2,000+ **Droaam** warriors (gnolls, ogres, minotaurs, trolls, medusas) under the **Daughters of Sora
Kell** hit the **Graywall** passes at dusk, capturing citadels **Mt. Anglio, Virtue Soars, Blackwood
Cleft** (3 of 13 built after the 990 Graywall Levy). Breland's **7th Army** marches from Galethspyre;
rumors the floating fortress **Argonth** may redeploy. Baroness **Avarulao** demands a "cleansing
crusade." **Hook:** hold a besieged citadel, escort refugees, or scout the horde's leadership.
(Source: Droaam-Hordes-Press-Graywall-Defenders.pdf)

## Therendor 8, 998 — "Lhazaar Investment Pacts Announced" (Zarash'ak)
**House Tharashk** (triumvir **Khundar'aashta**, **Aashta** family Finder's Guild) launches
dragonshard-prospecting ventures in the **Lhazaar Principalities**, partnering with **Prince Ryger
ir'Wynarn's Seadragons** and **Prince Kaelisk's Coldtide Fleet** (Stonespur Island) — surprising, as
Lhazaar was never a known shard source, yet a prospector returned with finger-sized shards in two
weeks. **Hook:** guard/sabotage a prospecting expedition; why is Lhazaar suddenly shard-rich?
(Source: Lhazaar-Investment-Pacts-Announced.pdf)

## Barrakas 8, 998 — "Aundair Army Captures Rebel Leader" (Wyr)
The **Ashbound** druid **Amleerin the Fox** is captured after a Wynarn-River battle, ending eight
months of raids that razed villages (leaving a glowing sigil on a tree) — worst at **Jarran** near
Windshire (Nymm), where 200+ died in their homes as if asleep. The **Wardens of the Wood** also
hunted him. He'll be tried at **Fairhaven**. **Hook:** rescue or interrogate Amleerin; investigate the
sleep-death sigil magic; break him out — or stop a breakout. (Source: Aundair-Army-Captures-Rebel-Leader.pdf)

## Nymm 8, 998 — "Relief Convoy Attacked in Western Aundair" (Wyr)
A 12-wagon aid caravan flying a **blue (noncombatant) flag**, organized by **Lord Egan ir'Ransaldrie**
for the village of **Salanoux**, is destroyed a mile short of its goal in the Aundair/**Eldeen Wolves**
crossfire; no survivors. Both sides blame each other; Wolves' leader **Rhunadask** denies it, calling
Aundair tyrants. Colonel **Borchard** (Otharaunt fortress) will secure the region. **Hook:** find who
really struck the convoy; broker or exploit the blame game. (Source: Relief-Convoy-Attacked-in-Aundair.pdf)

## Nymm 8, 998 — "'Dragonslayer' Sniper Claims Sixth Victim" (Sharn)
A crossbow sniper (a single magic bolt + a fiery corona scorching a black outline) kills a **House
Kundarak** dwarf elder, then posts taunting handbills (spread by *unseen servant*) railing against
Kundarak lending and the "dragontainted houses." Victims (all dragonmarked heirs): **Ilya d'Cannith**
(Nymm 2, Ashblack), **Endari d'Tharashk** (Dravago 20, Clifftop), **Lasca d'Ghallanda** (Dravago 17,
North Market), **Chenni d'Jorasco** (Dravago 11, Seventh Tower), **Torlak d'Sivis** (Dravago 4,
Callestan). Watch Commander **Belew Yorgan** and Sergeant **Harrina Thandalask** (Blackened Book) are
on it. **Hook:** hunt the anti-house terrorist — is it a lone zealot, an aberrant-marked avenger, or a
faction? (Source: Dragonslayer-Sniper-Claims-Sixth-Victim.pdf)

## Lharvion 8, 998 — "Lhazaar Volcano Erupts" (Regalport)
**Mount Cathanikau** erupts on an island ~500 mi NE of **Port Verge** (home to natives, a **kalashtar
refugee enclave**, and an **Onatar** shrine); ash/embers block ships; **House Lyrandar** + the
**Diresharks** stage an offshore relief convoy. Hierophant Druid **Tybalt Orenn** seeks magic to calm
the mountain. First major eastern eruption since the 881 YK **Jaurakhti Mountain** eruption. The island
is a rumored resting place of the lost pirate **Hoard of the Twin Drakes** (Drakes died 945). **Hook:**
run the rescue through lava and ash; find the hoard; save the kalashtar. (Source: Lhazaar-Volcano-Erupts.pdf)

## Rhaan 22, 998 — "Dream Serpent Hides Become Fashion" (Korranberg)
Capes of green-and-silver **dream serpent** hide from **Xen'drik** are the rage among **caravan
dancers**; seamstress **Chandri Thomm** started the trend; hides are scarce (serpents are deadly and
poisonous; **drow** treat hunters as poachers). Zilargan clothiers plan Xen'drik expeditions to
capture breeding stock. **Hook:** escort/guard a dangerous Xen'drik dream-serpent hunt; navigate drow
retaliation. (Source: Dream-Serpent-Hides-Become-Fashion.pdf)

## Sypheros 22, 998 — "House Lyrandar Mulls New Airship Routes" (Stormhome)
Scion **Yarrick d'Lyrandar** (aboard the *River of Stars*) negotiates new docking towers for
**Starilaskur, Sigilstar, Adderport, Keth,** and **Stormreach** (Xen'drik coast); rumored behemoth
airships (dwarfing the 300-ft standard) may follow, using Zilargo elemental-binding advances. **Hook:**
protect a tower-siting negotiation; escort the maiden run of a giant new airship. (Source: House-Lyrandar-Mulls-New-Airship-Routes.pdf)

## Aryth 22, 998 — "Banditry Delays Airship's Maiden Voyage" (Dragonroost)
Bandits from the **Howling Peaks** (36+ humans/goblinoids) raid the **Power of Purity's** Swiftwind
facility in NW Zilargo, burning a hangar with a nearly complete new high-speed airship. Founder
**Harkra Loivaerl Lonadar** demands Zilargo patrols. Lyrandar's exclusive-supplier talks with Power of
Purity may stall. **Hook:** hunt the Howling Peaks bandits; guard the rebuilt airship; uncover who
backs the raiders. (Source: Banditry-Delays-Airships-Maiden-Voyage.pdf)

## Eldeen Wolves campaign (Dravago 22, 998) — "Eldeen Wolves Seize Expedition" (Wyr)
The **Eldeen Wolves** under **Rhunadask** kidnap a **University of Wynarn** expedition (archaeologists,
porters, laborers), demanding Aundair quit the Wynarn Valley; **Queen Aurala** refuses to negotiate.
**Sentinel Marshals** (House Deneith) and the **Royal Eyes of Aundair** search secretly. Lord **Hauron
ir'Trellek** ("I've already given five sons") vows to hold the land. **Hook:** rescue the hostages;
infiltrate the Wolves; decide whether their cause is just. (Source: Eldeen-Wolves-Seize-Expedition.pdf)
