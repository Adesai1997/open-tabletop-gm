# Bestiary — Stat Blocks, NPCs & Monster Lookups

The **Fight Club** and **Character Class** articles provide fully-statted NPCs/monsters (3.5e) at
several Challenge Ratings — drop-in adversaries or allies. Cite the source file when used. For the
full stat lines (attacks, powers, gear), search the bundled full text with
`python3 scripts/lore_search.py "<name>"` (statblocks live in `lore/fulltext/fight-club-statblocks.md`); key
identity, role, and scaling are captured here.

## Ethereal Filcher Monk — "Taibo" (Source: Taibo-the-Ethereal-Filcher-Monk.pdf)
A self-trained **ethereal filcher** (aberration) monk who fled a life as a plane-hopping pickpocket to
become a silent, wise mountain guardian and teacher of fey. **Tactics:** drops in from the Ethereal
Plane, strikes with flurry for a few rounds, then jaunts away to recover — a master of attrition who
never lets foes rest or heal. Speaks no language. **Three versions:**
- **Taibo, 1st level — CR 4** (ethereal filcher monk 1; LN Medium aberration; AC 21, hp 45, 6 HD;
  darkvision 60 ft; Fort +5/Ref +7/Will +9; unarmed strike + bite; Dodge).
- **Taibo, 6th level — CR 9** (ethereal filcher monk 6).
- **Taibo, 12th level — CR 15** (ethereal filcher monk 12).

## "Imbrudar" — Brain in a Jar (Source: Imbrudar.pdf)
An undead **brain in a jar** psion crime-lord created in a lab, that dominated its creator and
fortified a hideout; it dominates or intimidates every creature in its sphere (a great crime boss).
Immune to a huge list (mind-affecting, crits, poison, disease, etc.); telepathy 100 ft; flies; usually
fights through a dominated minion (e.g. a giant). **Three versions:**
- **CR 7** — brain in a jar psion 2 (NE Tiny undead; AC 16, hp 57, 9 HD; 13 pp).
- **CR 14** — brain in a jar psion 9.
- **CR 18** — brain in a jar psion 13.

## "Bhissok" — Reptilian Tool of Tiamat (Source: Reptilian-Tool-of-Tiamat.pdf)
A **reptilian bugbear warmage**, a **Talon of Tiamat** bred by evil dragons in **Rethild** / the
**Farsea Marshes** to spread the Dragon Queen's influence. Confident (priests of Tiamat will raise him
if he falls), relies on warmage evocations early, melee later; keeps a *potion of fly* for escapes.
- **Bhissok, 6th level — CR 6** (reptilian bugbear warmage 3; NE Medium humanoid [goblinoid,
  reptilian]; AC 24, hp 43, 6 HD; darkvision 60 ft + scent; +8 vs evil-dragon frightful presence).
  Higher-level versions also provided in the article.

## "Beshappal" — the Vrock Berserker (Source: Beshappal-the-Vrock-Berserker.pdf)
An **advanced vrock barbarian** demon designed to stay relevant past 12th level (not the usual "flunky"
vrock). **CR 13** — advanced vrock barbarian 1; Male; hp 265 (17 HD); DR 10/good; abilities include
**Dance of Ruin** (three+ vrocks join hands and dance a lethal energy circle — stunning/paralyzing/
slaying one breaks it), spores, stunning screech, summon demon, rage 1/day.

## "Chuladoal" — Gravetouched-Ghoul Swarm-Shifter Troll (Source: Chuladoal.pdf)
A stacked-template horror: **fiendish gravetouched-ghoul swarm-shifter (pyro-)troll**, negative-energy-
healing with regeneration and turn resistance (Libris Mortis). Scales via added heads/pyro/barbarian:
- **CR 8** — troll/swarm form (hp 49, 6 HD; DR 5/magic).
- **CR 11** — four-headed pyro-troll (hp 94, 12 HD; DR 10/magic).
- **CR 15** — four-headed pyro-troll barbarian 4 (raging; hp 124, 16 HD; Uncanny Dodge).

## "Drashan Daverund" — Maur Servant of the Drow (Source: Drashan-Daverund.pdf)
A **maur** (a devolved, stooped storm giant of the Underdark) enslaved by a drow house; a **hulking
hurler** (Complete Warrior) who throws huge javelins and swings a huge warhammer, uses Power Attack,
and escapes with *meld into stone*. Accepts his slavery philosophically as paying an ancestral debt;
never sent out alone (drow warriors + a cleric/sorcerer guard him). **CR 13** at the featured build
(maur fighter 1/hulking hurler 1; NE Large giant [earth]; AC 27, hp 148, 14 HD; darkvision + low-light;
Common/Giant/Undercommon). Higher builds provided in the article.

## "Aleam Valassar" — Paladin Assassin (Source: Aleam-Valassar-Paladin-Assassin.pdf)
A **janni paladin** secretly possessed by the succubus **Lorycia** (via a fiend-of-possession
mechanic). While Aleam is awake he is a genuine paladin; when he sleeps, **Lorycia takes control**,
steering his training toward assassin skills. When she reveals herself, Aleam **loses paladin features
but gains assassin features** (each personality can use the other's skills/BAB/saves/feats but not the
other's class features). A perfect "traitor in the party's midst." Featured at **CR 6** (janni paladin
2, hp 70, 8 HD) with paired "Aleam in control" / "Lorycia in control" stat blocks; higher levels
provided.

## Tether Hound (Source: The-Body-Leech-Prestige-Class.pdf)
A guardian **construct** made of purplish-black ectoplasm, created by a body leech and bound to a
stasis cocoon. **CR 4**; Large construct; hp 63 (6d10+30); AC 17; bite 1d8+5 + 2 claws; DR 5/magic;
darkvision/low-light; understands Common & Draconic (mute). **Aurasense** (detects intruders within
its tether), **Tether** (30 ft per HD; a +1 slashing weapon can sever it — hardness 5, AC 17, 15 hp,
reattaches next round), psi-like **body adjustment** (as 8th-level psychic warrior). Attacks anything
in range that isn't the leech or another tether hound; fights to the death.

## Looking up rulebook monsters (SRD first, then bundled full text)
The library holds full monster books: **Monster Manual [2024]**, **Monster Manual** (2014),
**Monster Manual Expanded 1/2/3**, and **Fizban's Treasury of Dragons** (gem/metallic/chromatic
dragons). For a specific creature, run `python3 scripts/lookup.py monster "<name>"` first (SRD, offline), then search the bundled rulebook texts under `lore/fulltext/rulebooks/` with `python3 scripts/lore_search.py "<name>" --files rulebooks` (e.g. `rakshasa stat
block`, `couatl`, `beholder`, `medusa`, `sahuagin`) and read only the matching section. For
SRD-open creatures you may quote directly from `lore/fulltext/rulebooks/srd-ogl-5.1.txt` (see
srd-quick-tables.md and rules-supplements.md).
