## Conditions (Appendix A) — the effects a DM needs mid-session

- **Blinded** — Can't see; auto-fails checks requiring sight. Attack rolls against the creature have
  advantage; the creature's attack rolls have disadvantage.
- **Charmed** — Can't attack the charmer or target it with harmful abilities/effects. The charmer has
  advantage on social ability checks with it.
- **Deafened** — Can't hear; auto-fails checks requiring hearing.
- **Frightened** — Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of fear is in line
  of sight; can't willingly move closer to the source.
- **Grappled** — Speed 0 (no bonus to speed). Ends if the grappler is incapacitated, or if the
  creature is removed from the grappler's reach.
- **Incapacitated** — Can't take actions or reactions.
- **Invisible** — Impossible to see without special sense; heavily obscured for hiding. Attacks against
  it have disadvantage; its attacks have advantage.
- **Paralyzed** — Incapacitated; can't move or speak. Auto-fails Str and Dex saves. Attacks against it
  have advantage. Any hit from within 5 ft is a **critical hit**.
- **Petrified** — Turned to inanimate solid; incapacitated, unaware, can't move/speak; weight ×10;
  ages don't pass. Attacks against it have advantage; auto-fails Str/Dex saves; resistance to all
  damage; immune to poison and disease (existing poison/disease is suspended).
- **Poisoned** — Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
- **Prone** — Can only crawl (or stand, costing half movement). Disadvantage on its attack rolls.
  Attacks against it have **advantage** if the attacker is within 5 ft, otherwise **disadvantage**.
- **Restrained** — Speed 0. Attacks against it have advantage; its attacks have disadvantage.
  Disadvantage on Dex saving throws.
- **Stunned** — Incapacitated; can't move; can speak only falteringly. Auto-fails Str/Dex saves;
  attacks against it have advantage.
- **Unconscious** — Incapacitated; can't move/speak; unaware. Drops what it holds and falls prone.
  Auto-fails Str/Dex saves. Attacks against it have advantage; any hit from within 5 ft is a critical.

### Exhaustion (six levels; cumulative)
| Level | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Disadvantage on ability checks |
| 2 | Speed halved |
| 3 | Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws |
| 4 | Hit point maximum halved |
| 5 | Speed reduced to 0 |
| 6 | Death |
A long rest (with food and drink) reduces exhaustion by 1 level; effects end when level drops below 1.

|---------------|------------|
| 1 | ×1 |
| 2 | ×1.5 |
| 3–6 | ×2 |
| 7–10 | ×2.5 |
| 11–14 | ×3 |
| 15+ | ×4 |
(For a party of fewer than 3 characters, use the next-higher multiplier band; for 6+ characters, use
the next-lower band.)

## Ability Checks — the six abilities & their skills
- **Strength:** Athletics.
- **Dexterity:** Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth.
- **Intelligence:** Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion.
- **Wisdom:** Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival.
- **Charisma:** Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion.
- **Constitution:** no skills (used for concentration/exhaustion saves and hit points).
Add proficiency bonus if proficient; expertise doubles it. **Passive score = 10 + all modifiers**
(e.g. passive Perception).

## Proficiency Bonus by Level (PC or by monster CR band)
Levels 1–4: **+2**; 5–8: **+3**; 9–12: **+4**; 13–16: **+5**; 17–20: **+6**.

## Core resolution reminders (SRD)
- **d20 Test** = d20 + ability modifier + proficiency bonus (if applicable) vs DC or AC.
- **Advantage/Disadvantage:** roll 2d20, take higher/lower; they don't stack and cancel each other out
  regardless of how many sources apply.
- **Critical hit:** natural 20 on an attack roll auto-hits and lets you roll the attack's damage dice
  twice (add modifiers once). Nat 1 on an attack auto-misses.
- **Death saving throws:** at 0 hp, roll d20 (no modifiers); 10+ succeeds, 9 or lower fails; three
  successes stabilize, three failures die; nat 20 = regain 1 hp; nat 1 = two failures; taking damage
  at 0 hp = one failure (two if from a critical hit).
- **Concentration:** on taking damage, make a Con save, DC 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is
  higher; you can concentrate on only one spell at a time.
- **Cover:** half cover +2 AC/Dex saves; three-quarters cover +5; total cover can't be targeted
  directly.
- **Long rest** restores all hp and half your total Hit Dice; a creature can benefit from only one
  long rest per 24 hours.
