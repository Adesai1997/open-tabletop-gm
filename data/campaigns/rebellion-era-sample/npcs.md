## Darro Vel (Contact)

**Species:** Bothan | **Gender:** Male | **Age:** 40s
**Occupation:** Independent cargo broker / Rebel sympathiser
**Location:** The Grinding Wheel Cantina, Worlport

**Attributes:**
- Dexterity 2D+1 | Knowledge 3D+2 | Mechanical 2D | Perception 4D | Strength 2D | Technical 2D

**Key Skills:** bargain 5D, streetwise 4D+2, persuasion 4D+1, con 4D, search 3D+1

**Force Sensitive:** No | **Force Points:** 1 | **Character Points:** 3

**Personality:** Cautious, dry sense of humour, deeply loyal to people he's vouched for. Paranoid in crowds.

**Secret:** Darro is the Rebel cell's intelligence conduit. The cargo he's having the party deliver contains encrypted cell communications. He doesn't know the Empire has already flagged him.

**Quote:** *"I don't ask what's in the crates. You shouldn't either."*

## Inquisitor Veth Naraal (Antagonist)

**Species:** Human | **Gender:** Female | **Age:** 35
**Occupation:** Imperial Inquisitor (Force-sensitive agent)
**Location:** Arrives in session 2 (Complication beat)

**Attributes:**
- Dexterity 4D+1 | Knowledge 3D | Mechanical 3D | Perception 4D | Strength 3D+2 | Technical 2D+1

**Key Skills:** lightsaber 6D, dodge 5D, intimidation 5D, search 4D+2, sense 4D, control 3D+2

**Force Powers:** lightsaber combat, sense path, combat sense, danger sense, affect mind

**Force Points:** 3 | **Dark Side Points:** 4 | **Character Points:** 5

**Personality:** Ice cold professionalism with flashes of contempt. She does not negotiate. She collects.

**Quote:** *"You are not the target. Tell me where the target is, and this ends."*

**GM Note:** Do not introduce Naraal before the Complication beat. She should feel inevitable, not random.

## Prexis "Prex" Dhul (Ally)

**Species:** Sullustan | **Gender:** Non-binary | **Age:** 28
**Occupation:** Rebel cell technician / slicer
**Location:** Scrapyard District, Sector 9

**Attributes:**
- Dexterity 2D+1 | Knowledge 3D | Mechanical 3D+2 | Perception 3D | Strength 2D | Technical 5D

**Key Skills:** computer_programming 6D+2, astrogation 5D, security 4D+1, space_transports_repair 4D+1, search 3D+1

**Force Sensitive:** No | **Force Points:** 1 | **Character Points:** 3
