# World — Rebellion Era Sample Campaign

## Setting

**Primary Location:** Ord Mantell — a Mid Rim world controlled loosely by the Empire, notorious for crime syndicates, bounty hunters, and Imperial customs corruption. The Worlport city-zone is a dense warren of starport districts, cantinas, black markets, and industrial junkyards.

**Era:** ~1 BBY. The Death Star has not yet been destroyed. The Rebellion is fragmented and hunted. The Empire is confident, bloated, and brutal.

---

## Factions

| Faction | Disposition | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Galactic Empire / Project COBALT | Antagonist | Imperial Intelligence cell hunting Rebel sympathisers; reports to an Inquisitor |
| Rebel Alliance (Sabre Cell) | Potential ally | Underground cell operating out of a scrapyard; low on credits and trust |
| Zann Consortium | Neutral/threat | Crime syndicate; the party owes them money or favours |
| Darro's Network | Unknown | The contact the party is supposed to meet; his loyalties are ambiguous |

## Atmosphere Notes

- Ord Mantell smells like ozone, rust, and fried ration packs.
- The Empire is present but not omnipresent — local Moffs are corrupt and look the other way for credits.
- Betrayal is a live possibility from nearly every NPC. The Rebels are desperate. The Empire is ruthless.
- Tone: lived-in, morally grey, scrappy. Think *The Empire Strikes Back* Han Solo energy.
