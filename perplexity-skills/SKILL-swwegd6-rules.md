## Dice Convention

**Core resolution:** Roll a pool of six-sided dice (XD+Y notation) against a Difficulty Number.
Meet or beat the Difficulty = success. Under it = failure.

**Die codes:** `3D` = roll 3d6. `4D+2` = roll 4d6 and add 2. The number before D is the dice pool; +1/+2 is added to the total.

**The Wild Die:** One die in every roll is the Wild Die (different colour).
- Roll 2–5: add normally
- Roll 6: add 6, roll again — keep adding as long as 6s appear (exploding)
- Roll 1: GM chooses one — add normally, OR subtract Wild Die + highest die from total, OR add normally but a complication occurs

**Difficulty Numbers:**

| Difficulty | Range | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Very Easy | 1–5 | Driving on an empty road |
| Easy | 6–10 | Firing at medium range |
| Moderate | 11–15 | Repairing a starfighter |
| Difficult | 16–20 | Piloting through an asteroid field |
| Very Difficult | 21–30 | Decrypting Imperial military codes |
| Heroic | 31+ | Exhaust port shot |

**Opposed Rolls:** Both sides roll their relevant skill. Higher total wins. Ties go to the initiating character.

**Multiple Actions:** Each extra action in a round applies −1D penalty to ALL rolls that round.
- 2 actions: −1D to all
- 3 actions: −2D to all
- 4 actions: −3D to all

Reaction skills (dodge, melee parry, brawling parry) can be declared after being attacked — they count as an extra action for the rest of the round.

**Free actions:** Initiative roll, short shout, Strength roll to resist damage, grabbing a nearby item.

## Attributes

Six attributes, each rated in die codes. Human range at creation: 2D–4D per attribute (18D total to distribute).

| Attribute | Governs |
|---|---|
| Dexterity (DEX) | Agility, ranged combat, dodging, throwing |
| Knowledge (KNO) | Education, galactic lore, languages, streetwise |
| Mechanical (MEC) | Piloting, astrogation, gunnery, vehicle operation |
| Perception (PER) | Awareness, persuasion, deception, gambling, sneaking |
| Strength (STR) | Physical power, damage resistance, brawling |
| Technical (TEC) | Repair, programming, demolitions, first aid |

No modifier conversion. A Dexterity of 3D+2 means roll 3d6 and add 2 — directly.

**Knowledge:** alien species, bureaucracy, cultures, intimidation, languages, law enforcement, planetary systems, scholar, streetwise, survival, willpower

**Strength:** brawling, climbing/jumping, lifting, stamina, swimming

**Technical:** armor repair, blaster repair, capital ship repair, computer programming/repair, demolitions, droid programming, droid repair, first aid, ground vehicle repair, repulsorlift repair, security, space transports repair, starfighter repair, starship weapons repair, (A) medicine, (A) starfighter engineering

**Force Skills (Force-sensitive only):** control, sense, alter

Skills begin at their governing Attribute die code. Specializations add +1D for a specific use only.

**Advanced Skills (A prefix):** Require prerequisite skill at 5D+. Begin at 1D when purchased (not at Attribute level).

## Health & Damage

**Damage resolution:**
1. Attacker rolls weapon damage die code
2. Defender rolls Strength (armor adds dice for this roll only)
3. Damage − Strength difference determines wound level:

| Difference | Result |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Stunned |
| 4–8 | Wounded |
| 9–12 | Incapacitated |
| 13–15 | Mortally Wounded |
| 16+ | Killed |

**Wound track (cumulative):**

| Status | Penalty | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stunned | −1D this round + next round | Clears after 2 rounds; multiple stuns = unconscious 2D min |
| Wounded | −1D all rolls | Falls prone; can't act rest of round |
| Wounded Twice | −2D all rolls | Wounded again while already Wounded |
| Incapacitated | Unconscious 10D min | If wounded again → Mortally Wounded |
| Mortally Wounded | Unconscious; roll 2D/round or die | Roll 2D at end of each round; if roll < rounds mortally wounded, dies |
| Killed | Dead | Create new character |

**Stun weapons:** Any result worse than Stunned = unconscious 2D minutes instead.

**Armor:** Adds dice to Strength for damage resistance ONLY (not other Strength rolls).
- Stormtrooper armor: +2D physical, +1D energy, but −1D to DEX and all DEX skills

## Resources

**Force Points**
- Starting: 1 (non-Force-sensitive), 2 (Force-sensitive)
- Spend: Declare before rolling. Double ALL dice rolled that round. Cannot combine with Character Points in the same round.
- Using in anger or fear risks earning a Dark Side Point.
- Awarded by GM for heroic, selfless, or dramatically significant acts.

**Character Points**
- Starting: 5
- Spend in play: After a roll but before GM declares result — adds +1D to that roll. Cannot combine with Force Points same round.
- Spend between adventures: Improve skills (see Advancement).

**Dark Side Points**
- Gained for evil acts, killing non-combatants, using Force out of anger/selfishness.
- Each DSP grants +1D bonus to Force skill rolls — but accepting this bonus risks further DSP gain.
- If DSPs equal or exceed the character's Perception score (number before D), character turns to the dark side permanently.

## Recovery / Healing

**Medpac:**
- Requires first aid or Technical roll
- Difficulty by wound level: Stunned = Very Easy; Wounded = Easy; Wounded Twice = Moderate; Incapacitated = Difficult; Mortally Wounded = Very Difficult
- Success: heals one wound level
- Each additional medpac same day: raise difficulty one level

**Natural healing (slow):**
- Wounded: 3 days rest → Strength roll (2–4: worsens; 5–6: stays; 7+: healed)
- Incapacitated: 2 weeks → Strength roll
- Mortally Wounded: 1 month → Strength roll (failure = dies)

**Bacta Tank:**
- (A) medicine skill, Very Easy difficulty regardless of wound level
- Without (A) medicine: Heroic roll or wound worsens 2 levels

## Combat Sequence

**Round = ~5 seconds.**

1. **Initiative:** Each side's highest-Perception character rolls Perception. High roller chooses whether their side acts first or last. Reroll ties.
2. **Characters act in Perception order** (high to low) — both sides alternate by action count.
3. **Declare multiple actions before rolling** — apply penalties immediately.
4. **Reaction skills (dodge, parry):** Declare after being attacked; counts as extra action for rest of round.

## Scale

Objects and vehicles exist at different scales. Apply scale modifier as adjusted dice difference:

| Scale | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Character/Creature | +0D |
| Speeder | +2D |
| Walker | +4D |
| Starfighter | +6D |
| Capital Ship | +12D |
| Death Star | +24D |

Lower scale fires at higher scale: add modifier to attack roll; higher scale adds modifier to resistance.
Higher scale fires at lower: higher adds modifier to damage; lower adds modifier to dodge.

## Advancement

Between adventures, spend Character Points to improve skills:

| Advancement | CP Cost | Training Required |
|---|---|---|
| Improve skill +1 pip | Current # before D | 1 day per CP with teacher; 2× without |
| Improve attribute +1 pip | 10× current # before D | GM discretion |
| Learn new Force skill (1D) | 10 CP | 1 week intensive with teacher |
| Learn new Force power | 5 CP | Teacher or Holocron required |

3 pips = +1D (pips cycle: 0 → +1 → +2 → next die, e.g. 3D → 3D+1 → 3D+2 → 4D)

## Force Powers Reference

Characters must be taught a power to use it. Each skill used by a power is a separate action.

**Control:** Accelerate healing, control pain, detoxify poison, emptiness/hibernation trance, enhance attribute, resist stun, short-term memory enhancement

**Sense:** Combat sense, danger sense, life detection, life sense, magnify senses, receptive telepathy, sense Force, sense path

**Alter:** Injure/kill, telekinesis, bolt of hatred, electronic manipulation

**Control + Sense:** Farseeing, lightsaber combat, projective telepathy

**Control + Alter:** Accelerate another's healing, control another's pain, transfer Force

**Control + Sense + Alter:** Affect mind, battle meditation, dim another's senses

**Dark Side powers:** bolt of hatred, injure/kill — any power used with dark intent risks a DSP.

## Starship Combat

- **Piloting = Maneuverability** (starfighter piloting / space transports for dodge/evasion)
- **Gunnery** = starship gunnery + fire control vs. target's dodge/maneuverability
- **Shields:** Operator can redistribute points between arcs each round
- **Scale modifiers** apply (see Scale above)
- **Astrogation** roll required before jumping to hyperspace
