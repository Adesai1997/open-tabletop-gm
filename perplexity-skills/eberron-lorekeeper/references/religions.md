# Religions, Faiths & Monastic Orders

## Church of the Silver Flame
The most-detailed faith in the corpus (2-part *Dragonshards* series + the Purge article).

**Core beliefs** (Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1.pdf): The Silver Flame is not a
humanoid deity but a **celestial force of many noble spirits**, formed to bind the demon-spawn of
Khyber in the Dragon Below. It was "too pure for flawed humanity" until the warrior **Tira Miron**,
guided by a **couatl** (feathered serpent), gave her life to bind an escaped demon lord; her soul
joined the Flame, becoming the **Voice** humanity could hear. Worshipers serve the cause rather than
petition for favors. The dead "join the Flame" (so priests are loath to raise the dead). Couatl are
its chief allies. **Favored weapon: the longbow** (dates to fighting rakshasas, vulnerable to
piercing) (Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-2.pdf).

**Structure** (Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-2.pdf): Ministers/friars train, then attend
the **Great Seminary of Flamekeep**; templars train at fortress-monasteries (Kloijnir, Tira's Watch,
Morningcrest). Roles: **ministers/priests** (mostly experts/adepts), **templars** (warriors/fighters/
monks — the "Mironites"), **paladins** (a spiritual calling, held to the highest standard),
**friars** (missionaries and the Church's eyes), **pilgrims** (wanderers; the usual PC-cleric role).
Above them: **cardinals** and the **Keeper of the Flame** (Jaela Daran). Rank ≠ character level.

**Corruption** (three kinds, Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-2.pdf): (1) zealotry that
sacrifices mercy (torture/kill for the greater good); (2) greed (selling divine magic, seeking
power); (3) true darkness — the demon Tira bound whispers a "false flame," and some serve the **Lords
of Dust** from within the Church. A good-deity cleric always reads as a good aura regardless of
personal alignment, so *detect evil* is no license to kill.

**The Lycanthrope Purge** (Source: Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf): In Eberron a lycanthrope's
alignment is **not** tied to its animal form. Around **800 YK** the curse surged (afflicted could
spread it), overrunning western Khorvaire. Keeper **Jolan Sol** declared in **832 YK** that
lycanthropy corrupts the soul, launching the Silver Flame's inquisition into Aundair/Breland/Eldeen.
Paranoia killed hundreds of innocent **shifters** (who do *not* carry the curse). House **Medani**
dragonshard foci finally let templars find true lycanthropes; the Church withdrew by **880 YK**.
Modern moderates regret it, but **Aundair puritans** still show no mercy; Keeper **Jaela Daran** now
orders templars to subdue and cure/exile when possible under the Code of Galifar. Rumor: Jaela herself
may be a lycanthrope. Shifter moonspeakers say the moon **Olarune** created the first lycanthropes
from shifters, corrupted by a hidden thirteenth "dark moon."

## The Sovereign Host (the Vassals)
The mainstream pantheon of "the Vassals"; worship is often "somewhat lackadaisical," drawing disdain
from the Silver Flame's faithful (Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1.pdf). Named Sovereigns in
the corpus:
- **Boldrei** — hearth/community; the ranking priestess of Boldrei in Aundair is **Baltia Serontain**
  of Lathleer, who bears a Siberys-dragonshard holy symbol (Source: Faith-and-Deed.pdf).
- **Aureon** — law/knowledge; teachings echoed by the Church (Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1.pdf).
- **Dol Arrah** — honor/sunlight/war; warriors share ground with the Silver Flame (Source:
  Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1.pdf).
- **Dol Dorn** — strength and steel; patron of the **Order of the Broken Blade**; "stands between his
  honorable sister Dol Arrah and the treacherous Mockery" (Source: Monastic-Orders.pdf).
- **Onatar** — the forge/craft; shrines on Cathanikau Island and near the Fist of Onatar (Sources:
  Lhazaar-Volcano-Erupts.pdf; Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf).
- **Kol Korran** — trade/wealth; a dwarven favorite (Source: Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf).

## The Dark Six
The malevolent counterpart deities. Named:
- **The Mockery** — lord of pain, vengeance, treachery; patron of assassins and the monastic **Flayed
  Hand** (Source: Monastic-Orders.pdf).
- (The Shadow, the Devourer, the Fury, the Keeper, the Traveler complete the Six in canon; the corpus
  names the Mockery explicitly and references "the Dark Six" as a group — Source:
  Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1.pdf. Retrieve *Faiths of Eberron* at runtime for the rest.)

## Blood of Vol
Faith the Silver Flame seeks to abolish; strong in **Karrnath** (temples). Rooted in the ancient elf
**line of Vol**, which dabbled in blood magic/dark arts and was exterminated by the Undying Court and
allied dragons; the heir **Erandis d'Vol** allegedly escaped (though Lord Haensu of the Cairdal Blades
claimed to have slain her) (Sources: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1.pdf; House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf).
Adapt undead themes via *Libris Mortis* (Karrnathi skeletal armies, the "Queen of the Dead") (Source:
Libris-Mortis-Part-One.pdf).

## Cults of the Dragon Below (Khyber cults)
Worship the fiends/aberrations bound in Khyber; produce **wilders and ardents** drawing on madness and
raw emotion; revere aberrant-marked "children of Khyber" as blessed; tied to **Xoriat** manifest zones
(Sources: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf; The-Children-of-Khyber.pdf; Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1.pdf).

## The Undying Court (Aerenal)
The **deathless** ancestors of the Aereni elves, venerated as a divine council; formed after the
**Priests of Transition** won out over the blood-magic line of Vol and the martial **Tairnadal**.
Selected the first **Sibling Kings**; wears a golden mask (echoed by Phiarlan's silver mask). Fought
the first war against the dragons of Argonnessen (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf).

## The Path of Light (kalashtar)
The kalashtar faith opposing the Dreaming Dark; produces "bold paladin[s] of the Path of Light" as a
counterpoint to Dreaming Dark assassins (Source: Blades-of-the-Quori.pdf). Centered in **Adar**,
Sarlona (Source: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf).

## Druidic sects of the Eldeen Reaches (and beyond)
Five canonical sects, plus offshoots (Source: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf):
- **Wardens of the Wood** — guardians/mediators; warforged druids gravitate here.
- **Ashbound** — militant anti-arcane radicals; the arsonist **Amleerin the Fox** was one (Source:
  Aundair-Army-Captures-Rebel-Leader.pdf).
- **Children of Winter** — believe nature will scour the world with plague/disaster.
- **Gatekeepers** — protect the world from aberrations/daelkyr; bound the Daelkyr in Khyber; trained
  (per legend) by a dragon; revere the greatpine **Oalian** (Sources: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf;
  Heirs-of-Dhakaan.pdf).
- **Greensingers** — fey-touched revelers.
- Offshoots: **Shadows of the Forest** (King's Forest, Breland — displacer-beast affinity) and the
  Mournland **Followers of the Broken Path** (warforged healers) (Sources:
  Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf; Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).
**Druidic language** is a magical, unteachable tongue learned at 1st druid level — a way druids
identify each other; believed by some to be Eberron's primal first language (Source:
Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-One.pdf). Druids can **reincarnate** the worthy dead; the Eberron table
includes changelings/shifters and reflects strong orc druidic ties (you cannot be reincarnated *as* a
warforged) (Source: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf).

## Monastic orders (Source: Monastic-Orders.pdf)
Both religious and secular monastic traditions exist. Three detailed here:

### Order of the Broken Blade (Dol Dorn)
Founded from the legend of **Kalan Desh**, who slew three ogres with his bare hands and a shattered
sword hilt. Combat is meditation; enlightenment through conflict (members may fight on both sides of a
battle). Ranks: **initiate → swordbrother/sister → blademaster** (must forge his own sword; every
monastery has a forge). Primary monasteries: **Karrlakton, Rekkenmark** (Karrnath), **Starilaskur**
(Breland); an outpost at **Krona Peak** (Mror Holds). Close ties to the Karrnathi line of House
Deneith. Feats: Whirling Steel Strike, Weapon Focus/Improved Critical (longsword), Monastic Training.

### The Flayed Hand (the Mockery)
Monks embrace pain: they ritually **flay their own skin** and toughen the muscle, permanently marking
themselves; they become master torturers, assassins, and mercenaries who craft robes from victims'
skin. Ranks: **initiate → excoriate → archimandrite**. Trademark feat **Flensing Strike** (needs
Weapon Focus: kama). Primary monasteries: **Great Crag** (Droaam), **Rukhaan Draal** (Darguun);
elsewhere small and hidden. Marks recognized by any Host/Dark Six priest, or via Knowledge (religion)
DC 15 / bardic DC 20.

### The Shaarat'khesh ("Silent Knives")
Goblin spies/assassins from the ancient Dhakaani empire; now returned with the Heirs of Dhakaan.
Neutral mercenaries who serve all clans equally for gold (but won't assassinate clan leaders or dirge
singers). Ranks: **shaarul** (dull blade) → **tar'khesh** (silent hand, combat) / **il'khesh**
(silent eye, espionage) → **mula'duur** (assassins) → **guula'khesh** (silent master). Only true
monastery: hidden in the **Seawall Mountains**. Multiclass rogue/monk via Monastic Training. Known to
all Dhakaani; others need bardic knowledge or Knowledge (history) DC 25.

Other orders named: the **Mironites** of the Silver Flame and the **Tashalatora** of Adar (the
kalashtar psionic-monk tradition); the secular Valenar **Jaeldira** (Source: Monastic-Orders.pdf).
