# Psionics in Eberron

## Theory: Dal Quor & Xoriat (Source: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf)
One school of Eberron cosmology holds the planes are interwoven aspects of one reality, with every
living creature spiritually connected to many planes. Two planes especially touch mortal minds:
- **Dal Quor**, the region of dreams — binds to the imagination.
- **Xoriat**, the realm of madness — the font of all powerful emotions (rage, sorrow, joy).
If so, **psionic power is planar energy focused through the mortal mind**: the **wilder**'s psychic
surge draws on the raw emotion of Xoriat; the **psion**'s disciplined powers are "dreams forced upon
reality."

## Sarlona — the kingdom of the mind
Seat of psionic power; homeland of the **Inspired** and **kalashtar**, plus duergar, dromites, elans,
maenads, and half-giants. Adar elders believe Sarlona was once closely tied to **Dal Quor**,
explaining its many psionic creatures; those manifest zones faded when Dal Quor was thrown out of
alignment. A DM restricting psionics can require any psionic tie to trace back to Sarlona/Adar. Detail
was promised in *Secrets of Sarlona* (Source: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf).

## Psionics in Khorvaire
Khorvaire's psionic link runs through **Xoriat**: **mind flayers/illithids** (iconic Xoriat
aberrations) fought the Empire of Dhakaan with psionic weapons — treasures that could resurface today.
**Daelkyr** and illithids corrupt creatures, seeding "illithid heritage" traits anywhere. **Cults of
the Dragon Below** produce **wilders and ardents** drawing on madness. Ways to deepen psionics in a
campaign (Source: Psionics-in-Eberron.pdf):
- **Lhazaar Principalities** preserve old Sarlonan traditions — a good home for *Complete Psionic*'s
  **Six Hidden Houses**.
- **Quori inspiration:** kalashtar teach benevolently (Path of Light); the **Dreaming Dark** trains
  psions with hidden agendas (future host-bodies, soulknife assassin guilds, telepathic spies).
- **National security:** **Karrnath** may explore battlefield **kineticists**; **Breland**'s **King's
  Dark Lanterns** may train **lurks** to counter telepathic spies — risking Quori sleeper agents.
- **Removing psionics:** simplest is to strip the kalashtar/Inspired/Sarlona elements, or reflavor
  kalashtar as natural sorcerers (see races-cultures.md).

## Quori-warrior traditions (Source: Blades-of-the-Quori.pdf)
Quori-touched characters (kalashtar and Inspired) blend three classes — **monk, soulknife, psychic
warrior** — via three traditions (found on both sides; rivals show grudging mutual respect):
- **Jilashtora** ("flowing blades") — the most common; **soulknife + monk**. Feats: Mobility, Dodge,
  Combat Expertise, and the new **Flowing Blade** feat; skills Tumble and Perform (dance).
- **Ojilashta** ("bladeshapers") — **soulknife + psychic warrior**; uses *call weaponry* and powers
  like dissolving weapon, inertial armor, force screen, metaphysical weapon, prevenom weapon,
  vampiric blade to reshape/enhance the mind blade.
- **Tashalantora** ("fists of fluid thought") — **monk + psychic warrior**; mechanically the **Fist of
  Zuoken** PrC (Expanded Psionics Handbook); favors stealth powers (burst, chameleon, conceal
  thoughts). Related: the **Tashalatora** monastic tradition of Adar (Source: Monastic-Orders.pdf).

**Feat — Flowing Blade [General]** (Source: Blades-of-the-Quori.pdf): *Prereq:* ability to generate a
mind blade, Weapon Focus (mind blade), flurry of blows. *Benefit:* treat your mind blade as a special
monk weapon so you can flurry with it. *Special:* a kalashtar/Inspired monk may take it as a bonus
feat at 2nd or 6th level.

A quori's **mind blade** is an expression of the spirit within and can look like anything (razor
shards, black glass, a bolt of white light) with no mechanical change — good color for distinguishing
a Dreaming Dark assassin from a Path of Light paladin (Source: Blades-of-the-Quori.pdf).

## Sourcebook adaptations

### Complete Psionic in Eberron (Source: Complete-Psionic.pdf)
Part of Keith Baker's "Eberron Expanded" series adapting non-Eberron books. Introduces psionic options
(the **Six Hidden Houses**, ardents, lurks, etc.); Baker cautions the ideas are suggestions to fit
into Eberron rather than hard canon. Best seated in the Lhazaar Principalities or via Sarlonan contact.

### Expanded Classes, Part Four — psionic expanded classes (Source: Expanded-Classes-Part-Four.txt)
Covers PHB II-style **expanded class** options for the psionic classes from *Complete Psionic*,
including the **ardent, divine mind, lurk,** and **erudite** — alternate advancement paths layered on
the base psionic classes.

### Psionics of Incarnum (Source: Psionics-of-Incarnum.txt)
Merges **Magic of Incarnum** (soulmelds, essentia) with psionics — new feats, spells, psionic powers,
and prestige classes letting characters of all classes tap incarnum alongside psionic power.

## Psionic prestige classes & NPCs in the corpus
- **Psychic Theurge** and **The Body Leech** — full write-ups in prestige-classes.md.
- **Psionic monsters:** Dreamfane, Gruesome Lurker, Spryjack, Usunag (Four-New-Psionic-Monsters), plus
  Imbrudar the brain-in-a-jar psion — see bestiary.md.
- The **church of Sardior** (the Ruby Dragon, god of gem dragons/psionics) is cited as a natural home
  for psychic theurges (Source: Psychic-Theurge-Prestige-Class.pdf).
