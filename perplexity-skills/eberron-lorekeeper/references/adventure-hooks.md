# Adventure Hooks & Adventures

Distilled from the *Steal This Hook!* columns, the Dhakaani strike-force article, and the library's
5e adventures. (In-world 998 YK news hooks live in news-gazette.md.) Cite the source file when used.

## Steal This Hook! — Airship Action (Source: Airship-Action.pdf)
- **Lord of Airships:** two Lyrandar cargo airships vanished over the peaks north of Kennrun (Riedran
  crysteel, then adamantine). Lyrandar hires the PCs to fly a **decoy** airship; the **Lord of Blades**
  (who stole the first two) won't steal this one — he'll drive it into a lethal chasm gauntlet with
  warforged archers/wizards, then board with his two stolen ships.
- **Stowaways on the Wistful Wanderer:** Sharn's harbor is shut by sahuagin terror; the only ride to
  **Xen'drik** is the exclusive airship *Wistful Wanderer*. Dwarf **Jimodan Runabout** smuggles the
  PCs aboard as cargo — then is found with his throat slit mid-voyage.
- **Exploring the Dead-Gray Clouds:** in the Mournland sky, a lost Kythri air elemental **Aeba** has
  spun the dead-gray mist into a mythic storm; half-orc captain **Fled Grunlatch** needs a crew (for
  love/greed/revenge — a lost son, a legendary +4 longsword, or vengeance for his father).
- **Flight of the Nightfarer:** expelled changeling magewright **Arz** may have built an
  unsanctioned stealth airship (soarwood + elemental essences went missing from the Sharn Academy of
  Shipwrights). Framed innocent, saboteur, or genius?
- **Mini-hooks:** midair cargo transfer in a storm; stop an airship getaway with stolen letters of
  credit; Talenta halflings want a divine scholar to read a crashed airship; disguise a Thrane airship
  as a goblin air schooner en route to Droaam; a sea rescue of a burning elemental galleon carrying
  "throbbing cocoons" and spellwarped merfolk; escape an avalanche in an abandoned Frostfell airship;
  Karrnath recruits *feather fall* sky-warriors; harpoon down the airship carrying your Emerald Claw
  kidnappers with the help of a lammasu.

## Steal This Hook! — Mysterious Disappearances (Source: Mysterious-Disappearances.pdf)
- **The Case of the Locked Vault:** a Tharashk bounty hunter's warded dragonshard vault is robbed
  nightly despite guards — teleporter, ethereal filcher, tunnel, or illusory "treasure" whose spell
  durations are simply ending?
- **Runaway in the Reaches:** an Eldeen farm girl vanished with a cloak, an antique shield, and two
  weeks' rations after Sylvan-tongue letters — wooed by a satyr, recruited by moon-worshippers,
  kidnapped by fey, or sold to evil druids?
- **The Eyestalks Have It:** a kalashtar scholar's preserved-beholder-eyestalk collection is stolen;
  the door was chewed open yet the cases were carefully dispelled — a Silver Flame purist, a mad
  artificer, a demon-summoner, a blind monster, or a real beholder?
- **The Missing Mummy:** the sarcophagus of Galifar lord **Bruudash the Third** opens empty at a Sharn
  museum — collector's theft, a shriveling curse, or a mummy walking of its own accord?
- **One Carload Short of a Lightning Rail:** a private sleeping car vanishes from the Wroat→Sharn
  lightning rail in heavy fog, carrying Last-War veteran **Forv Yannar** en route to a rally —
  disintegration, teleport, time-frozen train, a roc, enemies, or a staged disappearance?

## Steal This Hook! — Mini-Hook Madness (Source: Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf)
40+ one-line Eberron seeds, e.g.: consult the greatpine **Oalian** over a burnt Gatekeeper tome;
**Prince Ryger** hires a probe of the Gray Tide mist (a possible unknown manifest zone); a loremaster
finds a PC named in the draconic **Prophecy**; meditate among the swirling dragonmarks of the **Seven
Caves** in Darguun; a changeling burned in Thrane sparks a changeling revolt; a chaos cult in the
**Shadow Marches** tries to break a **Kythri** dimensional seal; a disguised **rakshasa** at an
Aundair coronation; a lich's bones at auction in Sharn; the **Marguul** bugbears feint at Zilargo to
mask a bigger threat; a warforged serial killer in Korranberg; a Kundarak noble murder pits **Sivis**
vs a dwarven bankers' union at Korunda Gate; the **Chamber**, the **Aurum**, and **King Kaius** conspire
to put the party on a doomed Xen'drik ship; airborne carpet-gladiators over Sharn; **Ikar the Black**'s
salvage gang at New Cyre; a Gatekeeper investigates dragonshards funneled to an unknown partner in the
Marches; a slave trade at **Festering Holt** in the Demon Wastes.

## Steal This Hook! — Faith and Deed (religion hooks) (Source: Faith-and-Deed.pdf)
- **Symbolic Power:** in **Lathleer, Aundair** (on the House Orien road), ranking **Boldrei** priestess
  **Baltia Serontain** is found unconscious and her unique **Siberys-dragonshard holy symbol** is
  stolen. A d100 motivation table: she genuinely wants it back (led to it by Boldrei) / she's mildly
  corrupt and wants it back quietly / she's already dead and a **doppelganger** replaces her. Rewards
  for religious patrons can be spells, secret knowledge, or forbidden magic rather than just gold.

## Steal This Hook! — A Place to Call Home (Source: A-Place-to-Call-Home.pdf)
- **Flowing Fire:** in the **Mror Holds**, magma seeps into the mining city of **Vonmalk** near the
  **Fist of Onatar** (controlled by **Clan Soldorak** per the *Eberron Player's Guide*, or **Clan
  Mroranon** per the *Eberron Campaign Setting* — a noted canon conflict). Lord **Morrid Soldarak**
  suspects an evil hand behind the "natural" magma and hires PCs to descend. d100 motivations: he's
  honest / he's secretly allied with the creatures causing it for power / other. Reward: gold, or
  access to dwarven smiths for rare arms and armor.

Monster-fight hooks tied to *Monster Manual V* — e.g. **burrow roots** ambushing travelers in Furyondy

## Strike Force: Dhakaan (Source: Strike-Force_-Dhakaan.pdf)
Not a single adventure but a reusable **elite goblinoid strike force** to drop into any scene — as a
deadly ambush, an escort of unexpected allies, or a rival treasure-hunting party in a Dhakaani ruin.
See organizations.md for their composition, tactics, and weaknesses (poor Will saves, no divine magic).

## 5e adventures in the library
- **Princes of the Apocalypse** (Source: D-D-5e-Princes-of-the-Apocalypse.txt): four **elemental cults**
  rise in the **Dessarin / Sumber Hills** valley, each serving a **Prince of Elemental Evil** and a
  **Elder Elemental Eye**. Structure: **Ch. 1** Rise of Elemental Evil (Red Larch, valley sites);
  cult strongholds **Feathergale Spire** (air), **Sacred Stone Monastery** (earth), **Scarlet Moon
  Hall** (fire), **Rivergard Keep** (water); **Ch. 4** the four elemental temples (Howling Hatred /
  Black Earth / Crushing Wave / Eternal Flame); **Ch. 5** the Temple of the Elder Elemental Eye (Fane
  of the Eye, Howling Caves, Vale of Dancing Waters); prophet villains incl. **Aerisi Kalinoth** (air).
  Appendices: Genasi, spell lists, adapting to other worlds. **Eberron adaptation:** seat the four
  cults as **Cults of the Dragon Below** or elemental-bound conspiracies threatening a region.
- **Dragon Delves** (Source: D-D-5e-Dragon-Delves.txt): a 5e anthology of dragon-themed short
  adventures — good one-shots or side quests (retrieve for individual delves).
- **TARTH MOORDA** (retrieve at runtime; not extracted): a large adventure/location document — search
  for it with `search_files` when you need its content.
- **06 House Of Harpies** (Source: 06-House-Of-Harpies.pdf): a thieves'-guild lair hidden in a forest
  tree outside a town — a ready dungeon/locale (see organizations.md).
