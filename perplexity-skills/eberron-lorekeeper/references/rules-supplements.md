# Rules & Supplement Index

The library holds full rulebooks and crunch supplements. **Do not reproduce long verbatim passages
from copyrighted rulebooks** (2024 PHB, MM, DMG, Fizban's, PHB II, etc.) — distill mechanics in your
own words and point to runtime retrieval. **SRD/OGL content may be quoted** (see srd-quick-tables.md
and the bundled `SRD-OGL_V1.1-full.txt`). To pull a specific rule/spell/subclass, use `search_files`
with the exact term (it downloads the PDF to `/home/user/workspace/space_files/`), then `read` only
the relevant pages.

## Article-format crunch (distilled in sibling references)

- **Monks with Class.pdf**, **Sturdy Brawlers.pdf**, **Swashbucklers with Class.pdf**,
  **Swashbuckling 101: Combat.pdf** — martial character-build advice/options (retrieve at runtime;
  swashbuckler/brawler ones not extracted).

- **Intrigue and Betrayal.pdf** — noir tone/GM craft (world-primer.md).

  loss** and **ability-score loss** as scarier stakes than dying.

## The OGL SRD (open content — safe to quote)
- **`references/SRD-OGL_V1.1-full.txt`** — the complete System Reference Document 5.1 under the Open
  Game License v1.0a. Grep/read it for any open rule, condition, spell, class feature, magic item, or
  monster and quote it directly. Key ready-made tables are distilled in **srd-quick-tables.md**.
