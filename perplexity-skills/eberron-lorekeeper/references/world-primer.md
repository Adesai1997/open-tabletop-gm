# World Primer — Eberron 101

Distilled from the corpus. Where a fact is not attributable to an extracted file it is omitted;
retrieve `Religion in Eberron.pdf` and the rulebooks at runtime for deeper canon.

## Tone: noir meets pulp
Eberron is "a world of action and pulp adventure" but was also "inspired by the dark and uncertain
tone of film noir. It is a world where little is truly as it seems, where stories don't always end
well, and where allies can" betray you (Source: Intrigue-and-Betrayal.pdf). Run it with morally grey
factions, double agents, and hard choices rather than clean good-vs-evil. A recurring theme: **good
people can do evil and evil people can serve good** — the Church of the Silver Flame embodies this
paradox (Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-1.pdf).

Magic is widespread but true masters are rare: magewrights and adepts are common, but "true
artificers, wizards, and sorcerers are relatively rare — and high-level characters with these
classes are rarer still," so a 17th-level wizard is a genuine legend (Source: Masters-of-Magic.pdf).

## The Draconic Prophecy & dragonmarks
Dragonmarks are tied to the draconic Prophecy studied by dragons (Argonnessen) and the oldest
ascendant councilors of the Undying Court; the elves coined the term "dragonmark," though few
remember it (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf). Anything tied to the Prophecy is viewed with
suspicion. The **Mark of Death** appeared ~3,000 years ago on the line of Vol and led to that
line's extermination (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-One.pdf). **Aberrant dragonmarks** are a separate,
feared strain (see organizations.md, Children of Khyber).

## The Mourning and the Mournland
The Day of Mourning destroyed the nation of **Cyre**, turning it into the **Mournland** — a broken
land under a permanent "dead-gray mist." Healing magic generally fails there. The old capital of Cyre
was **Metrol**; House Phiarlan's original Demesne of Shape was in Metrol and was lost in the fall,
its master artisans "miraculously (some say suspiciously)" away on the Day of Mourning (Source:
House-Phiarlan-Part-Two.pdf). Warforged **Followers of the Broken Path** now try to heal the
Mournland (Source: Druids-of-Khorvaire-Part-Two.pdf). Cyran refugees resettled at **New Cyre**
(Source: Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).

## The Last War
A ~century-long war among the successor states of the kingdom of **Galifar**, ended (in the present
day) by the **Treaty of Thronehold**. House Deneith's **Sentinel Marshals** and mercenary Blademark,
the Karrnathi undead legions (Blood of Vol influence), and the Cyran catastrophe all trace to it.
The corpus references the war constantly: e.g. warforged were built by House Cannith foundries as
soldiers (Source: Guile-the-Warforged-Ninja.pdf); Aundair still fights guerrillas along the Wynarn
after the Eldeen Reaches won independence (Source: Eldeen-Wolves-Seize-Expedition.pdf); Breland
withdrew from western Breland in 987, letting the Daughters of Sora Kell seize what became **Droaam**
(Source: Droaam-Hordes-Press-Graywall-Defenders.pdf). Adapt mass-combat via *Heroes of Battle*
(Source: Heroes-of-Battle-Part-One.pdf).

## Galifar & governance
The old kingdom of Galifar predates the Five Nations; the **Code of Galifar** still authorizes the
Silver Flame templars to defend the Five Nations against supernatural threats such as lycanthropes
(Source: Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf). Prince Karrn ("Karrn's Conquest") once brought the Mror
dwarves under Galifar's peace and called them "barbarians" (Source: Dwarves-of-the-Mror-Holds-Part-2.pdf).

## The Galifar calendar — year 998 YK
The in-world news is all dated to **998 YK** (Year of the Kingdom). The corpus gives the month order
through its datelines (all Source: the news items in news-gazette.md):
1. **Zarantyr** (e.g. Zarantyr 6 & 8, 998)
2. **Olarune**
3. **Therendor** (Therendor 1 & 8, 998)
4. **Dravago** (Dravago 22, 998)
5. **Nymm** (Nymm 8, 998)
6. **Lharvion** (Lharvion 8, 998)
7. **Barrakas** (Barrakas 8, 998)
8. **Rhaan** (Rhaan 22, 998)
9. **Sypheros** (Sypheros 22, 998)
10. **Aryth** (Aryth 22, 998)
11. **Vult**
(Twelve 28-day months; Olarune and Vult are standard Eberron months not individually dated in the
extracted news but included for completeness.) **Olarune** is also named as one of Eberron's moons,
tied to the origin of shifters/lycanthropes (Source: Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf).

Days of the week appear in datelines/text: **Sul, Mol, Zol, Wir, Zor, Far** (e.g. "News for Sul,
Therendor 1st"; "attacked on Mol"; "announced on Zor"; "posted on Zol"; "as of last Wir") (Sources:
Droaam-Hordes-Press-Graywall-Defenders.pdf; Lhazaar-Volcano-Erupts.pdf; Banditry-Delays-Airships-Maiden-Voyage.pdf; Relief-Convoy-Attacked-in-Aundair.pdf). Eberron has **thirteen moons**;
shifter moonspeakers speak of a hidden **thirteenth "dark moon"** behind lycanthropic corruption
(Source: Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf).

## Currency & economy
Standard coinage appears throughout: **copper, silver, gold (gp), platinum (pp)**. Sample prices
from House Phiarlan's espionage services give a feel for scale (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf):
- A minor old secret: **10–100 gp**.
- Information obtainable by scrying: base **280 gp**.
- Long-term magical surveillance: ~**2,000 gp/day**.
- A complex field operation (e.g. a Dreadhold extraction): **tens of thousands of gp**.
Dragonshards (Eberron, Khyber, and Siberys shards) are a key magical resource and trade good; House
Tharashk's Finder's Guild prospects them (Source: Lhazaar-Investment-Pacts-Announced.pdf), and a
Siberys shard can empower a holy symbol (Source: Faith-and-Deed.pdf).

## Travel & the wonders of the age
- **Airships**: House Lyrandar's elemental-bound vessels connect the capitals; standard ships are
  ~300 feet, with rumored larger designs, built at yards like the **Power of Purity** in Zilargo.
  Routes and new towers (Stormreach, Adderport, Keth, Sigilstar, Starilaskur) are expanding
  (Sources: House-Lyrandar-Mulls-New-Airship-Routes.pdf; Banditry-Delays-Airships-Maiden-Voyage.pdf).
- **Lightning rail**: House Orien's magical trains link cities (Source: Mysterious-Disappearances.pdf;
  Repairs-Interrupt-Lightning-Rail.pdf — retrieve at runtime).
- **Sea travel**: Lhazaar sea-baron fleets (Seadragons, Diresharks, Coldtide) move cargo and
  explorers (Source: Lhazaar-Investment-Pacts-Announced.pdf; Lhazaar-Volcano-Erupts.pdf).

## Key institutions & places named in the corpus
- **Sharn, City of Towers** (Breland) — sprawling metropolis; wards include Mithral Tower, Clifftop,
  Callestan, Tradefair, Ashblack, Holdfast; policed by the Sharn Watch and its Blackened Book
  division; home to Morgrave University (Sources: Lyrandar-Investigates-Mysterious-Lights.pdf;
  Dragonslayer-Sniper-Claims-Sixth-Victim.pdf; Mini-Hook-Madness.pdf).
- **Library of Korranberg** & **University of Wynarn** — great centers of learning in Zilargo/Aundair
  (Sources: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf; Eldeen-Wolves-Seize-Expedition.pdf).
- **Arcanix** — Aundairian arcane academy (Source: Lycanthropes-and-the-Purge.pdf).
- **Dreadhold** — the great island prison (Source: House-Phiarlan-Part-Three.pdf).
- **Flamekeep** (Thrane) — seat of the Church of the Silver Flame (Source: Church-of-the-Silver-Flame-Part-2.pdf).
- **Stormreach** — Khorvaire's foothold on the Xen'drik coast (Source: House-Lyrandar-Mulls-New-Airship-Routes.pdf).
